# SERIES CONTENT STRATEGY

**Purpose:** Recurring content franchises that build audience, drive engagement, and create a reason to come back. Each series has a consistent title card, a consistent hook structure, and a built-in engagement mechanic ("let me know what you want next"). Episodes can be weekly, monthly, or triggered by comments/events.

**Why series work on TikTok:** The algorithm rewards watch time and engagement signals. A series creates a Pavlovian response — viewers who watched Episode 1 and found value will stop on Episode 2 the moment they recognize the title card or hook phrase. It also builds parasocial trust faster than one-off videos because the viewer feels like they are following a story, not watching ads.

### Series 2: "Pharmacist Counseling" *(Presentation format: Comment-Reply; pair with a confirmed hook)*

**Concept:** Comment-Reply is a confirmed presentation format, not one monolithic standalone hook. Put a **real viewer question or comment** on screen and answer it as if counseling at the pharmacy counter, then pair the reply with the confirmed hook that fits the specific question. The series title card reads "Pharmacist Counseling" or "Rx Counseling" in the corner. Every episode can use a different question, product, patient scenario, and underlying hook.

**Title Card:** "Pharmacist Counseling 💊" (text overlay, upper left, every episode)
**Text Hook:** The comment itself — displayed on screen for 1–2 seconds before you respond
**Verbal Hook:** "I got this question from [commenter name], and I want to answer it properly." Use the commenter name only when the comment is real and visible.

**Why it is a series:** The Comment-Reply presentation format signals that a viewer question can receive a real answer, which can support participation and future content selection. Dr. Faith used this presentation format in two retained GMV records ($28,969 and $26,340). The series branding turns a reply format into a recurring franchise while the selected underlying hook supplies the actual opening and structure.

**Engagement mechanic:** End every episode with: "If you have a question you want me to answer on the next Pharmacist Counseling episode, drop it in the comments below." This is the most direct comment-farming mechanic in the library — you are explicitly asking for the next episode's content.

**The "skeptical comment" rule:** Use skeptical or challenging comments when possible — "does this actually work or is it just hype?" is more powerful than enthusiastic ones. Answering skepticism builds more trust than answering enthusiasm.

## SERIES EXECUTION RULES

**The title card is non-negotiable.** Every episode of every series must have the same title card text in the same position on screen. This is how the algorithm and the viewer recognize the series. The title card is your brand within the brand.

**Episode numbering creates urgency.** "Episode 1" implies Episode 2 is coming. Viewers who liked Episode 1 will watch for Episode 2. This is the primary retention mechanic for series content.

**The engagement CTA is the series engine.** Every episode ends with an explicit ask: "Let me know what you want me to cover next." This does three things: (1) it generates comments, which feeds the algorithm; (2) it gives you your next episode's content; (3) it makes the viewer feel ownership over the series — they are co-creating it with you.

**Frequency guidance:**
- Series 1 (Pill Bottle), 2 (Counseling), 4 (Medication Depletion): Post as comments and questions present themselves — these are event-triggered, not calendar-driven.
- Series 3 (Gift Guide): Calendar-driven — Father's Day, Mother's Day, Christmas, Valentine's Day, Back to School.
- Series 5 (How Do You Know), 6 (Trend or Trash), 7 (Doctor Never Told You), 8 (Kids Health): Weekly or bi-weekly — these are evergreen and can be batched.

**Cross-series linking:** When a product appears in multiple series, reference the other episode. "I covered this in my Pharmacist Counseling series — link in my bio." This drives profile visits and increases follow rate.

**The comment reply as a series trigger:** Any comment that asks a question you have answered before is a Series 2 (Pharmacist Counseling) episode. Any comment that asks "what should I take for X?" is a Series 5 (How Do You Know) episode. Any comment that asks "is X worth it?" is a Series 6 (Trend or Trash) episode. Your comment section is your content calendar.
