## IMPORTANT NOTE: Two Versions Exist
- **Original version (SPF50+ PA++++):** Chemical UV filters — Diethylamino Hydroxybenzoyl Hexyl Benzoate (DHHB/Uvinul A Plus), Ethylhexyl Triazone, Methylene Bis-Benzotriazolyl Tetramethylbutylphenol (Tinosorb M), Diethylhexyl Butamido Triazone (Uvinul T 150). This is the Korean/EU formula with next-gen chemical filters not approved in the US.
- **UV version (SPF50 OTC — TikTok Shop listing):** US-compliant formula. Active ingredient: Butyloctyl Salicylate (chemical UV filter). This is the version being sold on TikTok Shop in the US.

## Active Ingredient (US OTC Version)
- **Butyloctyl Salicylate** — chemical UV filter, UVB protection. NOT a mineral sunscreen despite "Sun Serum" name.
- No zinc oxide, no titanium dioxide — this is a CHEMICAL sunscreen.

## Key Inactive Ingredients & Mechanisms
- **Centella Asiatica Extract** — soothing, anti-inflammatory. In original formula at 9,800ppm concentration. UV version concentration not disclosed.
- **Hyaluronic Acid / Hydrolyzed Sodium Hyaluronate / Sodium Hyaluronate** — multi-weight HA system for hydration
- **Panthenol (Vitamin B5)** — barrier support, soothing
- **Niacinamide** — NOT in the UV version (confirmed by Ulta ingredient list). Present in original formula.
- **Baby Green Complex** — rice, oat, soybean seed extracts — nourishing/antioxidant
- **Tocopherol (Vitamin E)** — antioxidant
- **Camellia Sinensis (Green Tea) Leaf Extract** — antioxidant
- **Portulaca Oleracea Extract** — soothing
