## 2. KEY INGREDIENT DEEP DIVE

### Kojic Acid — PRIMARY BRIGHTENING MECHANISM
- **What it is:** A naturally derived compound (from fermented rice/fungi) that inhibits the tyrosinase enzyme
- **Mechanism:** Tyrosinase is the enzyme that converts tyrosine → melanin. Kojic acid blocks this enzyme upstream — it prevents new melanin from forming before it starts.
- **Result:** Gradual lightening of existing dark spots + prevention of new pigmentation
- **Timeline:** Works upstream (prevention), so results take longer to appear vs. AHA exfoliation. Typically 4–8 weeks for visible results.
- **Clinical evidence:**
  > Wawrzyk-Bochenek I et al. "Evaluation of the Reduction of Skin Hyperpigmentation Using a Preparation Containing Kojic Acid."
  > *PMC 2023.* PMID: PMC10094916
  > **Finding:** Kojic acid preparation significantly reduced hyperpigmentation using hyperspectral imaging measurement.
  
  > Phasha V et al. "Review on the Use of Kojic Acid — A Skin-Lightening Ingredient."
  > *Cosmetics.* 2022;9(3):64. DOI: 10.3390/cosmetics9030064
  > **Finding:** Substantial evidence that kojic acid effectively inhibits melanin production and reduces pigment in melasma patients.
- **Pharmacist-only angle:** "Kojic acid blocks the tyrosinase enzyme — that's the enzyme that makes melanin. It stops new pigmentation before it forms."

### Ceramide NP — BARRIER REPAIR
- **Mechanism:** Ceramides are lipids that make up the skin barrier. Ceramide NP specifically restores the lipid matrix of the stratum corneum.
- **Why it matters for underarms:** Shaving disrupts the skin barrier. Ceramide NP repairs it while the product works. This is why Medicube is appropriate for people who shave frequently — it is actively repairing the damage that causes irritation and darkening.
- **Pharmacist-only angle:** "The ceramide repairs the skin barrier — so it's doing two jobs at once: brightening and healing."

### Centella Asiatica (Cica)
- **Mechanism:** Anti-inflammatory, wound healing, collagen synthesis support
- **Why it matters:** Reduces irritation from shaving. Soothes active bumps. Standard in Korean skincare for sensitive skin recovery.

### Shea Butter
- **Mechanism:** Emollient — seals moisture into the skin barrier. Supports ceramide function.
- **Why it matters:** Keeps the underarm skin hydrated and supple, reducing friction and irritation.

### Magnesium Hydroxide (Odor Control)
- **Mechanism:** Raises skin pH to create an environment where odor-causing bacteria cannot thrive. This is the "natural deodorant" mechanism — no aluminum, no sweat blocking, just pH adjustment.
- **Pharmacist-only angle:** "It controls odor by adjusting the pH of your skin — bacteria that cause odor can't survive in that environment. No aluminum needed."

### Turmeric Extract (Curcumin)
- **Mechanism:** Antioxidant and anti-inflammatory. Supports the brightening effect of kojic acid by reducing oxidative stress that can trigger melanin production.

### NO AHA/BHA — KEY DIFFERENTIATOR
- **Why this matters:** AHA (like in Truly Beauty) exfoliates by dissolving the bonds between dead skin cells. On freshly shaved skin, this can cause stinging and irritation. Medicube has zero AHA — it is safe to use immediately after shaving.
- **Pharmacist-only angle:** "There's no AHA in this formula — which matters if you shave. AHA on freshly shaved skin can sting. This was designed for sensitive underarm skin."

## 7. CONTRAINDICATIONS / INTERACTION FLAGS

- **Kojic acid sensitivity:** Small percentage of people develop contact dermatitis. Patch test recommended.
- **Not an antiperspirant:** Will not stop sweating. Manages odor only. Set expectations.
- **Pregnancy:** Kojic acid is generally considered safe topically but limited data in pregnancy. Recommend consulting OB.
- **No major drug interactions** at topical application levels.
