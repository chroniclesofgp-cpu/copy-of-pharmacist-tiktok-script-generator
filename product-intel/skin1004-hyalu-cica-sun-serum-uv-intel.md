## Active Ingredients (FDA OTC Drug Facts — DailyMed Verified)

| Ingredient | Percentage | UV Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Homosalate | 13.6 g/100 mL | UVB |
| Octocrylene | 9 g/100 mL | UVB + some UVA |
| Octisalate (Ethylhexyl Salicylate) | 4.5 g/100 mL | UVB |
| Avobenzone (Butyl Methoxydibenzoylmethane) | 2.7 g/100 mL | UVA |

**This is a 100% chemical sunscreen.** No mineral filters. The "UV" version uses the standard US FDA-approved filter system. The original Korean version used Diethylamino Hydroxybenzoyl Hexyl Benzoate (DHHB), Ethylhexyl Triazone, and Methylene Bis-Benzotriazolyl Tetramethylbutylphenol (Tinosorb M) — none of which are FDA-approved for US OTC use.

**Avobenzone stability note:** Avobenzone is the only FDA-approved UVA filter that provides full UVA coverage, but it is inherently photounstable — it degrades in UV light. Octocrylene in this formula acts as a photostabilizer for avobenzone, preventing degradation and maintaining UVA protection throughout wear. This is a standard and effective pairing.

## Key Inactive Ingredients (Pharmacist-Relevant)

**Centella Asiatica Extract (9,800 ppm)** — This is the brand's hero ingredient. At 9,800 ppm (approximately 0.98%), this is a meaningful concentration. Centella Asiatica contains madecassoside and asiaticoside — triterpenoid saponins with documented anti-inflammatory, wound-healing, and collagen-stimulating properties. Particularly relevant for post-sun soothing and for reactive/sensitive skin types.

**Sodium Hyaluronate (10 ppm) + Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid + Hyaluronic Acid (0.01 ppm)** — Three forms of hyaluronic acid at different molecular weights. Sodium hyaluronate (low molecular weight) penetrates the upper layers of the skin. Hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid is even smaller and penetrates deeper. Full-size hyaluronic acid sits on the surface and forms a moisture-retaining film. The layered approach provides hydration at multiple skin depths.

**Niacinamide** — Vitamin B3. Anti-inflammatory, reduces TEWL, supports barrier function. Particularly relevant for post-sun inflammation.

**Polymethylsilsesquioxane** — Silicone-derived spherical particles that give the formula its silky, serum-like texture and contribute to the no-white-cast, blendable finish.

**Caprylyl Methicone** — Silicone emollient. Gives the lightweight, non-greasy feel.

**Adenosine** — Anti-aging ingredient with documented anti-inflammatory properties. Helps reduce redness and supports skin repair.

**Tocopherol (Vitamin E)** — Antioxidant. Protects against free radical damage from UV exposure.

**Panthenol (Pro-Vitamin B5)** — Soothing, reduces TEWL, supports barrier function.

**Glycerin** — Humectant. Core moisturizing ingredient.

**Betula Platyphylla Japonica Bark Extract, Ginkgo Biloba Leaf Extract, Camellia Sinensis Leaf Extract** — Antioxidant botanical complex. Green tea (Camellia sinensis) has the strongest evidence base for antioxidant skin protection.

## Important Caveats for Scripts

1. **This is a chemical sunscreen.** Do not imply or state it is mineral. Be accurate.
2. **Homosalate controversy:** There is ongoing discussion about homosalate's potential endocrine disruption at high concentrations. The evidence is weak (animal studies at very high doses), but it is a known talking point in the sunscreen community. A pharmacist should be aware of this and either address it proactively or avoid making strong safety claims about the filter system.
3. **Avobenzone degradation:** Without photostabilization, avobenzone degrades and loses UVA protection. Octocrylene stabilizes it in this formula — but this is why reapplication every 2 hours is critical, not just for SPF but for UVA protection.
4. **Reformulation disclosure:** If viewers ask about the original Korean version, be honest that the US version uses different UV filters due to FDA regulations.
