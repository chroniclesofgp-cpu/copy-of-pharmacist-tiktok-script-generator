## Mechanism Deep Dive

**PDRN — The Pharmacist's Ingredient**
PDRN (Polydeoxyribonucleotide) is a registered pharmaceutical drug in South Korea and Europe, used clinically for wound healing, tissue repair, and anti-inflammatory treatment. Its primary mechanism: binds to adenosine A2A receptors → inhibits NF-κB and MAPK inflammatory pathways → promotes VEGF expression (angiogenesis) → stimulates collagen synthesis by downregulating Fli1 and upregulating CTGF → inhibits melanogenesis by reducing tyrosinase activity.

**Critical caveat:** The clinical studies on PDRN primarily involve injectable forms or high-concentration medical-grade topical applications. The concentration in a cosmetic product is significantly lower. A pharmacist should validate the mechanism as real while being honest that the cosmetic concentration produces a milder effect than the clinical literature suggests.

**Exosomes — The Emerging Technology**
Exosomes are nano-sized extracellular vesicles (30–150nm) secreted by cells. They carry proteins, lipids, mRNA, and microRNA. In skin applications: promote keratinocyte and fibroblast proliferation, modulate immune response, reduce inflammation, regulate collagen production and degradation, and deliver bioactive cargo to target cells. The Lacto Exosome in the Exosome Shot is derived from Lactobacillus fermentation.

**Critical caveat:** Topical exosome penetration without a delivery mechanism (like microneedling) is limited. The REBORNIC calcium spicule technology in the Exosome Shot is the key — the micro-channels created by the spicules allow the exosomes to penetrate beyond the stratum corneum. This combination (spicule delivery + exosome cargo) is the most pharmacologically interesting angle in the entire set.

**REBORNIC Calcium Spicule Technology**
Calcium spicules derived from sea sponge (Hydrolyzed sponge) create micro-channels in the stratum corneum. The ionized calcium in the spicules activates fibroblasts via calcium-dependent signaling pathways. Vitamin D in the REBORNIC formulation activates VDR (Vitamin D Receptor) → promotes keratinocyte differentiation and skin barrier repair. The micro-channels also dramatically increase penetration of co-applied actives (exosomes, niacinamide, AHA/BHA).

**The AHA + BHA + PHA Triple Exfoliant (Zero Pore Pads)**
- AHA (Lactic Acid) — water-soluble, surface exfoliation, calcium chelation disrupts corneocyte adhesion
- BHA (Salicylic Acid) — oil-soluble, penetrates pores, dissolves sebum plugs, anti-inflammatory
- PHA (implied by Panthenol + Allantoin context) — gentler exfoliation, antioxidant, suitable for sensitive skin
Three different exfoliant classes in one pad, each targeting a different layer and mechanism.

## Safety Flags and Honest Caveats

- **Spicule irritation:** Calcium spicules cause temporary redness, tingling, and sensitivity. Not suitable for broken skin or active rosacea flares. Patch test required.
- **PDRN concentration:** Cosmetic concentrations are lower than clinical/injectable concentrations. The mechanism is valid; the magnitude of effect is more modest than medical literature suggests.
- **Exosome penetration:** Without the spicule delivery, topical exosome penetration is limited. The Exosome Shot is the product where this matters most — and the spicule technology addresses it.
- **Multi-product complexity:** 8 products with AHA, BHA, PDRN, exosomes, retinol (if present), and peptides creates potential for over-exfoliation or irritation if all used simultaneously. Introduce gradually.
- **PDRN source:** Derived from salmon DNA. Individuals with fish allergies should be aware.
- **Fragrance:** Multiple products in the set contain Fragrance. Sensitive skin users should patch test.
