# PRODUCT INTEL: Celsius Energy Drink
**Protocol Level:** Light  
**Last Updated:** May 23, 2026  
**Role in Content:** Secondary product — used as the losing product in the Loaded Tea vs. Celsius comparison script  
**Format:** 12oz RTD can  
**Price:** ~$2.50–$3.00/can

---

## 1. KEY FACTS (Light Protocol — Verified)

**Caffeine:** 200mg per can (from green tea extract + guarana)  
**Sweetener:** Sucralose  
**Format:** Fixed 12oz can — no dose control  
**Key vitamins:** B vitamins, Vitamin C, Chromium  
**Proprietary blend:** "MetaPlus Blend" — green tea extract, guarana, ginger root, taurine, glucuronolactone  
**Calories:** 10 per can  
**No:** Sugar, artificial colors, high fructose corn syrup

---

## 2. WHY IT LOSES TO LOADED TEA (In the Comparison Script)

The comparison is not about ingredient quality — Celsius has a legitimate formula. The comparison is about format and dose control:

- **Fixed dose:** 200mg caffeine in a 12oz can. You drink it or you don't.
- **No dose control:** Cannot split the dose. Cannot sip over time without the drink going flat.
- **RTD format:** Convenient but inflexible.
- **Loaded Tea wins on:** Powder format (32oz, dose control), higher B-vitamin stack at higher doses, MK-7 K2 form, D3+K2 synergy.

---

## 3. HONEST PHARMACIST NOTES

- Celsius is a legitimate, well-formulated energy drink. It is not a bad product — it just loses on the specific comparison points (dose control, B-vitamin stack depth).
- The "MetaPlus Blend" is a proprietary blend — exact doses of each ingredient are not disclosed.
- Celsius has been involved in FTC settlement (2022) regarding marketing claims about calorie burning. This is public record. Do not bring it up unless directly asked — it is not relevant to the comparison video angle.
- Guarana is a natural caffeine source — the total caffeine from green tea + guarana combined is the 200mg listed.
