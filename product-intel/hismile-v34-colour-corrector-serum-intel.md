## 8. Pharmacist-Only Angle & Safety Flags

### Pharmacist-only angle

The key authority point is not that V34 is “better than whitening.” It is that **optical colour correction and chemical bleaching solve different problems**. A viewer can reasonably choose V34 for temporary appearance, but should not be told it removes intrinsic discoloration, repairs enamel, or replaces a dental assessment.

### Contraindications / interaction flags

- **Drug interactions:** None identified from the verified label.
- **Use boundaries:** Do not swallow; follow the label. Stop and seek dental/pharmacist advice if oral irritation occurs.
- **Clinical escalation:** Unexplained single-tooth darkening, pain, swelling, bleeding gums, or a sudden colour change requires dental assessment—not a brightening product.
- **Restorations:** Do not promise matching/whitening of crowns, veneers, bonding, implants, or fillings without product-specific official confirmation.

## 9. Verified Usage Instructions

**Sources checked:** Official [HiSmile U.S. page](https://us.hismileteeth.com/products/colour-corrector); official-seller [Amazon listing](https://www.amazon.com/Hismile-Corrector-Whitening-Toothpaste-Correcting/dp/B09LH36816); CVS label panel.

- **Frequency:** Brand page positions it for daily use / reapply as needed.
- **Application method:** Apply **two pumps** to a soft-bristle toothbrush; brush gently in a circular motion for **two minutes**; expel, do not swallow, and rinse excess formula from mouth.
- **Sequence:** Use as a cosmetic brightening step; no verified requirement to combine with strips or use after a specific toothpaste in the same session.
- **Clinical trial protocol:** Cotton-bud application for 30 seconds then rinse in the single-use RCT. This is **study protocol, not a replacement for package directions**.
- **Pharmacist application note:** The result is visually temporary, so a product-use tutorial must never promise a treatment course or cumulative shade change.
