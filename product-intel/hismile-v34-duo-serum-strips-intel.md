## 8. Pharmacist-Only Angle & Safety Flags

### Pharmacist-only angle

The credible message is **role separation, not forced synergy**: serum = temporary cosmetic colour correction; strip = 30-minute PAP/V34 treatment format. The absence of a verified combined sequence is itself a reason to instruct viewers to follow each label rather than improvised influencer protocols.

### Safety / usage flags

- **Drug interactions:** None identified from the verified label.
- **No unverified routine:** No same-day use order, treatment cadence, “serum after strips” claim, or 2–3x effectiveness claim.
- **Dental referral cues:** Oral pain, swelling, bleeding, persistent sensitivity, sudden one-tooth darkening, or unexplained discoloration are not bundle problems to self-treat.
- **Restorations:** Do not promise whitening/matching of veneers, crowns, bonding, implants, or fillings.

## 9. Verified Usage Instructions

### Serum

- Apply two pumps to a soft-bristle toothbrush; brush gently in circular motions for two minutes; expel, do not swallow, and rinse excess formula.
- Official brand position: daily/reapply as needed temporary brightening.

### V34 Whitening Strips

- Dry teeth; apply long strip to upper teeth and short strip to lower teeth; fold excess behind teeth.
- Leave for 30 minutes; remove and rinse / brush away purple residue as indicated in the official cart.
- 14 applications / 28 strips. Do not state an unverified day-by-day schedule.

### Bundle protocol status

> **No verified serum-plus-strip order exists for this exact SKU.** A Type 3 instruction-correction video must be built around each component’s verified instructions and explicitly avoid prescribing an unsupported combined sequence.
