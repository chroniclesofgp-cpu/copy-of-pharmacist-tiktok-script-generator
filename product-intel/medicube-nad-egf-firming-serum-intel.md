## Mechanism Deep Dive

**The NAD+ + CD38 Inhibitor System — The Unique Pharmacist Angle**
This is the most pharmacologically sophisticated NAD+ skincare formulation on the market. The problem with topical NAD+: even if NAD+ penetrates the skin, the enzyme CD38 (a NAD+ glycohydrolase) rapidly degrades it before it can reach the cell. Most NAD+ skincare products ignore this.

Medicube's solution: include Quercetin (a flavonoid) and Enoxolone (18β-glycyrrhetinic acid, derived from licorice root) as CD38 inhibitors. These two compounds inhibit CD38 activity, slowing NAD+ degradation and allowing more NAD+ to reach skin cells. This was validated in PMID 39513906 (Kang et al. 2024, *Cells*) — the first study to demonstrate that CD38 inhibition by quercetin and enoxolone significantly boosts the anti-aging effects of exogenous NAD+ in skin cells.

This is a pharmacist-only angle. No skincare creator can explain the CD38 degradation pathway credibly. It is also the most honest version of the NAD+ story — it acknowledges the bioavailability challenge and explains exactly how the formula addresses it.

**EGF (sh-Oligopeptide-1) and the Growth Factor Complex**
sh-Oligopeptide-1 is recombinant human EGF. It binds to EGF receptors (EGFR) on keratinocytes and fibroblasts → activates tyrosine kinase signaling → promotes cell proliferation, migration, and differentiation. In wound healing: well-established (clinical use in diabetic ulcers). In cosmetic anti-aging: promising but the evidence base is developing — most trials are uncontrolled. A pharmacist should present this accurately.

The serum also contains sh-Oligopeptide-2 (IGF-1), sh-Polypeptide-1 (bFGF), sh-Polypeptide-3 (VEGF) — a full growth factor complex. bFGF promotes fibroblast proliferation and collagen synthesis. VEGF promotes angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation). This is a pharmaceutical-grade ingredient stack.

**Copper Tripeptide-1 (GHK-Cu)**
One of the most studied cosmetic peptides. Promotes collagen I and III synthesis, elastin, and proteoglycans. Has wound-healing properties (used in clinical wound care). Also has antioxidant activity and promotes angiogenesis. The copper ion is essential — it activates lysyl oxidase, the enzyme that cross-links collagen fibers to give them structural strength.

## Safety Flags and Honest Caveats

- **EGF evidence:** Promising but not definitive for cosmetic anti-aging. Present accurately — strong for wound healing, developing for anti-aging.
- **Growth factors and oncology:** There is a theoretical concern that growth factors (EGF, bFGF, VEGF) could stimulate abnormal cell growth. The concentrations in cosmetic products are far below therapeutic doses, and no clinical evidence of risk from topical cosmetic use exists. A pharmacist should not raise this unprompted, but should be prepared to address it if asked.
- **Fragrance:** Listed — potential irritant for sensitive skin.
- **NAD+ topical bioavailability:** The CD38 inhibitor system improves this but does not fully resolve it. Present the mechanism accurately without overclaiming.
