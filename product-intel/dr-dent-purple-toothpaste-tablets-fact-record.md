### Evidence interpretation boundary

No product-specific clinical trial, quantified PAP level, hydroxyapatite particle-size disclosure, whitening magnitude, whitening durability, cavity-prevention result, sensitivity result, or no-irritation result was located for this exact 90-tablet product. ## 9. Pharmacist Value and Use Facts

| Fact opportunity | Source-grounded clarification | Boundary that must remain intact |
|---|---|---|
| **Optical correction versus bleaching** | The PDP positions purple tint as color theory that counteracts yellow appearance during brushing. The ADA distinguishes surface-stain removal from chemical bleaching of internal tooth color. | Do not describe purple tint as proof of a lasting whitening-treatment effect. |
| **PAP evidence versus exact formula** | PAP is on the physical label. Available cited PAP studies evaluate different gels, concentrations, contact times, and laboratory conditions. | Do not convert PAP research into a finished Dr. Dent tablet result. |
| **10% HAp versus nano-HAp research** | The tin claims 10% hydroxyapatite, while one relevant RCT studied different **nano**-HAp toothpastes. | Do not call this tablet nano-HAp, a sensitivity treatment, a cavity-prevention product, an enamel-repair product, or a fluoride equivalent/superior product. |
| **Directions are a demonstration fact** | Chew to paste; brush two minutes; spit and rinse. | Do not state or imply it can be swallowed; the package explicitly says not to swallow. |
| **Population and oral-health limits** | The package advises a dentist consultation for oral-health conditions and excludes pregnancy/breastfeeding and users under 16. | No age/pregnancy/breastfeeding targeting, and no dental diagnosis or treatment substitution. |
