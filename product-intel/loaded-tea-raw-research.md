## Key Ingredient Analysis

### Caffeine — 200mg from Green Tea
- Natural source (green tea extract)
- NOT caffeine anhydrous (synthetic)
- Green tea caffeine is absorbed differently — smoother onset
- Note: L-theanine is NOT listed separately on label. The caffeine is "naturally sourced from green tea" but the L-theanine content of the extract is not confirmed. Do NOT claim L-theanine as a benefit of this product.

### B-Vitamin Stack (pharmacist highlight)
- B3 (Niacin): 190% DV — supports energy metabolism, NAD+ production
- B5 (Pantothenic Acid): 260% DV — essential for CoA synthesis, fat metabolism
- B6 (Pyridoxine): 290% DV — neurotransmitter synthesis, amino acid metabolism
- B9 (Folic Acid): present — DNA synthesis, cell division
- B12 (Methylcobalamin): 400% DV — **KEY DIFFERENTIATOR: methylcobalamin is the active, bioavailable form** vs. cyanocobalamin (synthetic form used in Bloom). This is a pharmacist-level insight.
- D3 (Cholecalciferol): 100% DV — immune function, bone health, mood regulation
- K2 (Menaquinone-7/MK-7): present — **KEY DIFFERENTIATOR: MK-7 is the most bioavailable form of K2**. Directs calcium to bones, not arteries. Almost no energy drinks include K2.

## Pharmacist-Only Angles

1. **Methylcobalamin vs. Cyanocobalamin** — Loaded Tea uses methylcobalamin (active form). Bloom uses cyanocobalamin (synthetic form). Most people don't know there's a difference. This is a legitimate pharmacist insight that favors Loaded Tea.

2. **Menaquinone-7 (MK-7)** — The most bioavailable form of Vitamin K2. Almost no energy drinks include K2 at all. This is a genuine differentiator.

3. **200mg caffeine from green tea** — Natural source, smoother than synthetic. Cannot claim L-theanine specifically but can reference the natural source advantage.

4. **Dose control** — 32oz per packet. Can drink half, save half. Cannot do this with a 12oz can. This is a structural advantage for caffeine-sensitive users.

5. **Zero calories, zero carbs** — Keto-friendly. Bloom has 10 calories and 2g carbs. Small difference but worth noting for strict keto users.

## Contraindications / Pharmacist Flags

1. **High B-vitamin doses** — At 290% B6 and 400% B12, these are well above RDA. Not harmful for most people but those with B6 sensitivity (rare) should note. At these doses B vitamins are therapeutic, not just supplemental.
2. **Sodium: 350mg per packet** — 13% DV. If someone is sodium-restricted (heart failure, hypertension), this is worth noting. Not a disqualifier but a flag.
3. **Caffeine 200mg** — Standard caution: not for pregnant women, those with heart conditions, or caffeine sensitivity. Same as any energy product.
4. **Sucralose** — Same concern as Bloom.
