## Pharmacist Flags
1. Sucralose — artificial sweetener. Ironic given the prebiotic fiber claim — sucralose has been shown to alter gut microbiota in some studies. The prebiotic partially offsets this but worth noting.
2. Potassium Benzoate + Vitamin C — in the presence of ascorbic acid, benzoate can form benzene. The amount is typically below regulatory concern but it is a real chemistry interaction.
3. Cyanocobalamin vs. methylcobalamin — cyanocobalamin is the cheaper, less bioavailable form of B12. Not harmful but not optimal.

## Oligonol Study (verified)
- Citation: Kang SW et al. J Clin Biochem Nutr. 2012;50(2):106-113. DOI: 10.3164/jcbn.11-46
- Design: Double-blind RCT, 70 male participants, 800mg OLFE x 30 days
- Key finding: 7.4% increase in anaerobic threshold (p=0.01)
- On-screen text: "Patented lychee extract — 30 clinical trials — 7.4% endurance increase (RCT, 2012)"
