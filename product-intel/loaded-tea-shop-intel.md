## 2. KEY INGREDIENT DEEP DIVE

### B-Vitamin Stack — THE DIFFERENTIATOR
- **B12 at 400% DV:** Methylcobalamin form preferred. At this dose, it is actively supporting cellular energy metabolism (ATP production via methylation cycle), not just hitting a minimum. Most energy drinks hit 100% DV — 400% is a meaningful pharmacist talking point.
- **B6 at 290% DV:** Supports neurotransmitter synthesis (serotonin, dopamine, GABA). At this level, it is doing neurological work, not just checkbox nutrition.
- **B3 (Niacin) at 190% DV:** Supports NAD+ production — the coenzyme that powers mitochondrial energy production. This is the "cellular energy" angle.
- **Pharmacist-only angle:** "At these doses, this is actually doing something for your cellular energy metabolism — not just giving you a caffeine hit." This line is the key differentiator vs. Bloom and vs. standard energy drinks.

### Vitamin K2 (MK-7 Form)
- **Why MK-7 matters:** MK-7 (menaquinone-7) is the most bioavailable form of K2. Most supplements use MK-4 which has a half-life of hours. MK-7 has a half-life of 3 days — stays active longer.
- **Function:** Directs calcium to bones and away from arteries. Cardiovascular + bone health.
- **Pharmacist-only angle:** Naming MK-7 specifically signals you read the label. Most creators just say "K2."

### Vitamin D3
- Standard immune and bone health support. Pairs with K2 — D3 increases calcium absorption, K2 directs where it goes. This is a synergistic pair that most people do not know about.

### Green Tea Extract (Caffeine Source)
- Natural caffeine source. Green tea naturally contains L-theanine alongside caffeine.
- **IMPORTANT CAVEAT:** Green tea extract is processed — L-theanine content varies by extraction method. Loaded Tea does NOT list L-theanine as a separate ingredient. Do NOT claim L-theanine benefits for Loaded Tea.
- **Safe on-camera line:** "200mg of caffeine from green tea extract — natural source, natural form. That's already a different experience than synthetic caffeine."

### Aloe Vera
- Digestive soothing, anti-inflammatory. Supports gut lining. Mild prebiotic effect.
- Not as clinically robust as Sunfiber (Bloom's prebiotic) — do not overclaim.

### Inositol
- Supports insulin signaling and mood regulation. Often used in PCOS management. Mild anxiolytic properties.
- Pharmacist angle: "Inositol supports insulin signaling — that's part of why the energy feels stable, not spiky."

## 8. CONTRAINDICATIONS / INTERACTION FLAGS

- **Caffeine-sensitive individuals:** 200mg per full packet. Can use half packet.
- **High-dose B vitamins:** B6 at 290% DV — long-term very high B6 intake (>100mg/day) can cause peripheral neuropathy. At this dose level, occasional use is fine. Daily use over months warrants monitoring.
- **Warfarin / blood thinners:** Vitamin K2 can interact with warfarin. Important pharmacist flag.
- **Sucralose sensitivity:** Small population.
