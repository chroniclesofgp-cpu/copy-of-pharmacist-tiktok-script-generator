## 2. KEY INGREDIENT DEEP DIVE

### Rebornic™ — PATENTED — PRIMARY DIFFERENTIATOR

**INCI name (verified from UL Prospector):** Butylene Glycol (and) Water (and) Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis (Sweet Almond) Fruit Extract (and) Tabebuia Impetiginosa Bark Extract (and) Tremella Fuciformis (Mushroom) Extract

All three botanical extracts (Sweet Almond Fruit Extract, Tabebuia Impetiginosa Bark Extract, Tremella Fuciformis Extract) are present in the Multi Balm's ingredient list — confirming Rebornic is in this product.

**What it is:** A patented botanical extract complex from GSI EXIM America, Inc. It is NOT a spicule ingredient. It contains no needles, no micro-channels, and produces no tingling sensation.

**Mechanism (verified from UL Prospector):** Rebornic activates Vitamin D Receptor (VDR) expression in skin cells. VDR activation influences calcium absorption at the cellular level and promotes skin cell differentiation — the process by which new skin cells mature and replace old ones. This accelerates the skin's natural turnover cycle, helping to clear dead cells and support the emergence of newer, healthier skin.

**The pharmacist-only angle:** "The 'calcium' in this product isn't just a mineral sitting on your skin. Rebornic activates the receptor that controls how your skin cells absorb and use calcium — and that receptor, the Vitamin D Receptor, is what drives skin cell turnover. When turnover is working properly, you get clearer texture, better firmness, and more even tone. That's the mechanism behind the marketing language."

**What the brand says vs. what it means:**
- Brand says: "Patented calcium ingredient" → Means: Rebornic activates VDR to enhance calcium-driven cell differentiation
- Brand says: "Boosts collagen and elasticity" → Means: Improved cell turnover supports the skin's natural collagen maintenance cycle
- Brand says: "Reborn with young skin" → Means: Accelerated turnover replaces aged, sluggish skin cells with newly differentiated ones

### Adenosine — WELL-DOCUMENTED ANTI-WRINKLE INGREDIENT

**What it is:** A naturally occurring nucleoside found in all living cells. In skincare, it is a well-established cell-communicating ingredient with strong anti-wrinkle evidence.

**Mechanism:** Adenosine binds to receptors in skin cells and promotes collagen and elastin synthesis. It also has anti-inflammatory properties. It is one of the few topical anti-aging ingredients with robust clinical evidence for wrinkle reduction.

**Pharmacist-only angle:** "Adenosine is one of the most underrated anti-aging ingredients in skincare. It's not trendy, it doesn't have a flashy marketing story, but the clinical data on wrinkle reduction is solid. Most people have never heard of it. It's in this balm."

**Supporting study:** Tada A et al. "Adenosine increases anagen hair growth and thick hair in Japanese women with female pattern hair loss: a pilot, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial." *J Dermatol.* 2018. (Adenosine's cell-signaling role is well-established across multiple dermatology applications.)

### Collagen Extract + Hydrolyzed Elastin

**What they are:** Surface-level firming and hydration ingredients. Hydrolyzed elastin is broken into smaller fragments that can interact with the skin surface.

**Important limitation to state accurately:** Full-size collagen molecules cannot penetrate the skin barrier — they are too large. Collagen Extract and Hydrolyzed Elastin in this formula work primarily at the skin surface to improve texture, hydration, and suppleness. They do not penetrate to the dermis. This is NOT a limitation unique to this product — it applies to all topical collagen products.

**Pharmacist framing:** "The collagen in this balm works at the surface — it improves texture and hydration. The deeper collagen-building work is done by Rebornic through the cell turnover pathway, not by the collagen extract itself."

---

### Tremella Fuciformis (Snow Mushroom) Extract

**What it is:** A natural polysaccharide extract from snow mushroom. Often compared to hyaluronic acid for its water-binding capacity.

**Mechanism:** Holds up to 500x its weight in water. Smaller molecular size than standard HA allows better skin penetration. Provides significant plumping and hydration.

**Pharmacist-only angle:** "This balm doesn't have hyaluronic acid — it has Tremella mushroom extract, which does the same job but with smaller molecules that can actually get into the skin. It's the hydration workhorse in this formula."

---

### Calcium Carbonate (CI 77220)

**What it is:** A mineral compound (chalk/limestone). In this formula it functions as a texture agent and mild opacifier. It is NOT a bioactive calcium delivery system.

**Important clarification:** The "calcium" in the product name and marketing refers to Rebornic's VDR-activation mechanism — not to Calcium Carbonate as a standalone active. Calcium Carbonate is a supporting ingredient, not the mechanism driver.
