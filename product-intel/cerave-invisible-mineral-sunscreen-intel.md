## Key Inactive Ingredients (Pharmacist-Relevant)

**Ceramide NP, Ceramide AP, Ceramide EOP** — The three essential ceramides in CeraVe's signature blend. These are the same ceramides found naturally in the skin barrier (ceramides make up ~50% of the stratum corneum lipid matrix). Ceramide NP is the most abundant in human skin. Restoring these after UV exposure helps maintain barrier integrity.

**Niacinamide** — Vitamin B3. Reduces transepidermal water loss (TEWL), calms inflammation, and has documented efficacy for post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation. Relevant for acne-prone skin that also needs sun protection.

**Glycerin** — Humectant. Draws water into the skin. Clinically shown to maintain hydration at concentrations as low as 3%.

**Panthenol** — Pro-vitamin B5. Soothing, reduces TEWL, increases moisture in the uppermost skin layer.

**Cholesterol + Phytosphingosine** — Lipid components that work alongside ceramides to restore the skin barrier lipid bilayer. Phytosphingosine also has mild antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties.

**Diethylhexyl Syringylidenemalonate (DESM)** — This is the key to understanding the Miner-ALL technology. DESM is a photostabilizer and antioxidant that prevents UV filter degradation. It quenches singlet oxygen, which is a reactive species generated when UV filters absorb UV radiation. Without a photostabilizer, mineral filters can degrade over time with sun exposure, reducing efficacy. DESM keeps the formula performing throughout the day. (Verified study: PMID 16492073 — Sayre et al. 2006, *Photochemistry and Photobiology*)

**C15-19 Alkane** — Vegetable-derived emollient (replaces silicones). Gives the lightweight, non-greasy feel. Readily biodegradable.

**Ethylhexyl Methoxycrylene** — A chemical UV filter booster and photostabilizer. NOTE: This is a chemical UV filter component, not an active. It helps stabilize avobenzone-type filters and boosts SPF. Some very sensitive skin individuals or those with chemical sunscreen allergies may react to this. This is a legitimate pharmacist talking point — the product is marketed as "mineral" but contains this chemical stabilizer.

**MVE Technology (Multivesicular Emulsion)** — CeraVe's proprietary slow-release delivery system for moisturizing ingredients. Provides sustained hydration throughout the day rather than a single-burst release.

## Miner-ALL Technology Explained (For Scripts)

Zinc oxide at 15.35% would normally cause a significant white cast because zinc oxide particles scatter visible light. Miner-ALL technology addresses this through:

1. **Particle surface treatment** — Triethoxycaprylylsilane coats the zinc oxide and titanium dioxide particles, making them more compatible with the oil phase of the formula and reducing clumping.
2. **Silica dispersion** — Silica in the formula helps distribute particles evenly, preventing aggregation that causes white cast.
3. **DESM photostabilizer** — Prevents particle degradation during UV exposure, maintaining transparency throughout wear.
4. **Alumina coating** — Aluminum Stearate and Alumina provide an additional surface treatment layer on the mineral particles.

The result: a 15.35% zinc oxide formula that blends transparent. This is genuinely novel for a drugstore mineral sunscreen at this price point.

## Consumer Review Themes

**Positive:** No white cast confirmed across multiple skin tones. Lightweight feel. Works under makeup. Non-greasy. Good for sensitive skin. Hydrating.

**Negative:** Some users report a faint smell (described as "chemical" or "sunscreen-like"). A small number report breakouts (likely from Ethylhexyl Methoxycrylene or the emulsifier system). 3.1 stars on Ulta (older listing, fewer reviews) vs 4.6 on TikTok Shop — the Ulta rating may reflect the older hydrating mineral formula, not this new one.

**Important nuance:** One reviewer noted the product "comes out as a faint yellow-green color" but blends transparent. This is the DESM photostabilizer — it has a slight color in the tube that disappears on skin. Worth addressing proactively in scripts to prevent confusion.
