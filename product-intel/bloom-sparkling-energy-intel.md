## 2. KEY INGREDIENT DEEP DIVE

### Sunfiber® (Galactomannan) — PATENTED ™
- **What it is:** Partially hydrolyzed guar bean fiber — a water-soluble prebiotic fiber
- **Mechanism:** Feeds beneficial gut bacteria (Lactobacillus, Bifidobacterium); slows gastric emptying; stabilizes blood sugar response
- **Key differentiator:** Clinically shown to be well-tolerated — does NOT cause bloating, gas, or GI distress that most prebiotic fibers cause. This is a real differentiator.
- **Pharmacist-only angle:** Most consumers avoid prebiotic supplements because of the bloating side effect. Sunfiber is the exception. Naming it specifically is a credibility signal.
- **On-screen text:** "Sunfiber® — the prebiotic fiber that doesn't cause bloating"

### Oligonol® (Lychee + Green Tea Polyphenol) — PATENTED ™
- **What it is:** Proprietary oligomerized polyphenol blend from lychee fruit and green tea, manufactured by Amino Up (Japan)
- **Mechanism:** Increases nitric oxide production → improves blood circulation → reduces fatigue, improves endurance, cardiovascular support
- **Clinical evidence:** 30+ human clinical trials
- **Key study for on-screen popup:**
  > Kang SW et al. "Oligomerized lychee fruit extract (OLFE) and a mixture of vitamin C and vitamin E for endurance capacity in a double blind randomized controlled trial."
  > *Journal of Clinical Biochemistry and Nutrition.* 2012;50(2):106–113. DOI: 10.3164/jcbn.11-46
  > **Finding:** 7.4% increase in anaerobic threshold (p=0.01) in 70 male participants over 30 days. Double-blind RCT.
- **On-camera line:** "The patented lychee extract — backed by 30 clinical trials — increases nitric oxide, which improves circulation and reduces fatigue."
- **Do NOT say "Oligonol" on camera** — name means nothing to viewers. Say "patented lychee extract."
- **Dose caveat:** Study used 800mg. Bloom does not disclose the dose. Do not claim Bloom delivers the same effect size — just cite the mechanism and the research.

### L-Theanine (100mg) — CONFIRMED ON LABEL
- **Mechanism:** Promotes alpha brain wave activity → calm focus without sedation; counteracts caffeine jitter/crash
- **Ratio:** 100mg L-theanine : 200mg caffeine = 1:2 ratio (research-supported optimal range)
- **Key study:**
  > Giesbrecht T et al. "The combination of L-theanine and caffeine improves cognitive performance and increases subjective alertness."
  > *Nutritional Neuroscience.* 2010;13(6):283–290. PMID: 21040626
  > **Finding:** 97mg L-theanine + 40mg caffeine improved attention during demanding cognitive tasks vs. caffeine alone.
- **Pharmacist-only angle:** The 1:2 ratio is the studied ratio. Most energy drinks have caffeine only. Bloom has both in a meaningful ratio. This is the "clean energy" differentiator.

### Sucralose — HONEST CAVEAT
- **Issue:** Sucralose is an artificial sweetener. Emerging research suggests it may negatively impact gut microbiome at high doses. The irony: Bloom adds a prebiotic (Sunfiber) while also including sucralose which may partially counteract gut benefits.
- **On-camera use:** This is a nuanced pharmacist point — use it as a "here's what to know" moment, not a dealbreaker. Bloom also uses stevia. The dose of sucralose in one can is low.

## 7. CONTRAINDICATIONS / INTERACTION FLAGS

- **Caffeine-sensitive individuals:** 200mg is a full dose. Not appropriate for caffeine-naive people, pregnant women, or those with arrhythmia/hypertension.
- **MAOIs:** Caffeine + MAOIs can cause dangerous interactions. Standard caffeine warning.
- **Sucralose sensitivity:** Small population has GI sensitivity to sucralose.
- **No major drug interactions** with L-theanine or Sunfiber at these doses.
