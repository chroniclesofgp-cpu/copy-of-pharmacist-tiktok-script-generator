### Evidence boundaries

- [Stübinger et al., 2024 — PMID 38881836](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38881836/) describes a **professional PAP-complex technique**, not this consumer V34 strip. It supports only general PAP mechanism discussion.
- [Pascolutti & de Oliveira, 2021 — PMID 34940045](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34940045/) is an **in-vitro PAP+ enamel/stain model** using six 10-minute exposures. It cannot be translated to an individual V34 strip outcome. The authors were affiliated with HiSmile Research Centre / a company connection.
- The official product page’s “96% of participants had 2+ shades whiter teeth” statement is **brand test material**. Keep it labeled as brand-reported; do not present it as an independently verified published clinical trial.
- **On-camera-safe line:** “These are not just purple strips—the colour correction gives a temporary visual boost, while PAP is the stain-targeting part of this formula.”

## 8. Pharmacist-Only Angle & Safety Flags

### Pharmacist-only angle

The pharmacist differentiator is **evidence sorting**: separate brand-reported V34-strip results from published general PAP mechanism evidence, and separate immediate violet optical correction from the strip course. That correction prevents the common false conclusion that one post-strip look proves a permanent result.

### Contraindications / interaction flags

- **Drug interactions:** None identified from the verified formula.
- **Oral-care caution:** Do not claim suitability for every buyer with sensitivity, gum disease, damaged enamel, orthodontic appliances, or restorations. Check package warnings and consult a dentist/pharmacist if irritation or oral pain occurs.
- **Escalation:** Pain, swelling, bleeding, persistent sensitivity, a single suddenly dark tooth, or unexplained discoloration warrants dental assessment—not a cosmetic strip.
- **Dosing limit:** 14 applications are verified; no standalone daily-use schedule was independently confirmed for this exact current V34 SKU. Follow the package instructions rather than scripting “daily.”

## 9. Verified Usage Instructions

**Sources checked:** Official [HiSmile U.S. V34 Strips page](https://us.hismileteeth.com/products/v34-whitening-strips); official [TikTok Shop V34 Duo](https://shop.tiktok.com/us/pdp/hismile-v34-teeth-whitening-strips-serum-set-14-uses/1731607602950542096); detailed [retailer label transcription](https://hismile.online/en/hismile-v34-whitening-strips-brilliant-smile/p1752053831).

- **Preparation:** Dry teeth.
- **Application:** Apply the long strip to upper teeth and the short strip to lower teeth; fold excess behind teeth.
- **Wear time:** Leave in place for **30 minutes**.
- **Finish:** Remove and rinse. The official TikTok listing also says to brush off purple residue.
- **Course:** 14 applications / 28 strips; do not state a day-by-day schedule beyond the package unless a current official package verifies it.
- **Bundle order:** No official serum-plus-V34-strip sequence was found. Do not invent a same-session order, a “strip then serum” rule, or a combined maintenance schedule.
