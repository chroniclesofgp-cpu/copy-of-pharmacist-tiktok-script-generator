## How They Differ

### 1. Authority Framing
- **Faith** leads with "Doctor" in the title overlay and uses "I don't gatekeep" — positions herself as a trusted insider sharing hidden knowledge
- **Creator 2** leads with "18-year pharmacist AND skincare brand founder" — stacks two credentials, making him uniquely qualified to judge both the science AND the formulation

### 2. Comparison Technique
- **Faith** uses **split-face application** — applies Melaxin to the right side of her face, Medicube to the left, in real time. This is a physical demonstration that creates visual proof and keeps the viewer watching to see the result
- **Creator 2** uses **rapid ingredient stacking** — holds each product and fires off ingredients with color-coded text popups. No application demo, but the visual density of the ingredient list makes Medicube look overwhelmingly superior on screen

### 3. Dr. Melaxin Treatment
- **Faith** gives Melaxin a fair shake — describes it as good for "structure and firmness," applies it to her face, and acknowledges it works for a specific use case. The comparison feels more balanced before the winner reveal
- **Creator 2** gives Melaxin credit but moves through it quickly — the ingredient list for Melaxin is shorter, and the visual pacing makes it feel like a setup for Medicube's overwhelming ingredient dump

### 4. Medicube Ingredient Depth
- **Faith** focuses on 2 hero ingredients: **volufiline** (volume) and **PDRN** (collagen stimulation). Clean and memorable
- **Creator 2** lists 10+ ingredients rapidly with text popups. More impressive visually, but harder to retain verbally

### 5. Social Proof
- **Faith** uses no sales numbers — relies on the "launch price" urgency and 2-pack deal
- **Creator 2** uses hard numbers: 341K sold, 6,000 five-star reviews, "filler in a stick" viral claim

### 6. Tone
- **Faith** is warmer, more conversational, slightly more beauty-influencer energy
- **Creator 2** is clinical, fast-paced, pharmacist-authoritative — closer to your positioning

## Key Observations for Your Version

1. **The hook is identical in both videos** — "Product A vs Product B, [Credential] Explains / Which is better?" This is the proven format. Do not deviate from it.

2. **Your credential is stronger than both.** "PharmD" or "I'm a pharmacist" is the right authority line. You do not need to add skincare brand founder — your clinical framing is the differentiator.

3. **Split-face application is the visual anchor.** Faith's technique is the most distinctive element of her video and likely why it performs. You should replicate this — Melaxin right side, Medicube left side.

4. **Give Melaxin a fair shake.** The credibility of the comparison depends on not dismissing Melaxin immediately. Acknowledge it works for firmness, then show why Medicube wins on volume + ingredients.

5. **Use Creator 2's ingredient list structure for Medicube** — the rapid-fire text popups stacking volufiline, PDRN, collagen, EGF, peptides etc. is more visually impressive than Faith's 2-ingredient summary.

6. **Use Creator 2's social proof** — 341K+ sold and "filler in a stick" are proven viral claims that should stay in your version.

7. **The winner should be unambiguous.** Both videos end with Medicube winning clearly. Your version should do the same.
