# Bad/Good Label Demonstration — Completed Fact-Record First-Test Candidate Screen

**Purpose:** Identify the strongest first real-product test candidates for the user-approved synthesized `bad-good-label-demonstration` format.  
**Format card:** `analysis/rebuild-frameworks/BAD_GOOD_LABEL_DEMONSTRATION_SYNTHESIZED_TEST_CARD_2026-09-06.md`  
**Screening scope:** Every product record currently titled a completed **Fact Record** under `product-intel/`: DR.DENT Purple Toothpaste Tablets, DryWater Complete Daily Electrolyte & Vitamin Powder (Raspberry Lemon), and medicube Bye-Bye Bumps Red Body Set. Earlier `*-intel.md` files were not treated as current full fact records because they are not identified as completed, current-protocol Fact Records in the Clean-Slate Active Source Map.

> **Decision:** DryWater is the clearest first test. DR.DENT is a workable second test if the viewer goal is limited to a short, portable daily-brushing routine—not a whitening-efficacy comparison. Medicube Bye-Bye Bumps should **not** be forced into this format at present; its strongest fit remains the approved one-person bundle-value / routine-clarity format.

## 1. Eligibility Standard Used

Every candidate was screened against the synthesized-card prerequisites:

| Requirement | Decision rule |
|---|---|
| Label-visible difference | The distinction must be verifiable on the exact current product’s label, Nutrition/Supplement Facts, official directions, or an equally direct package/official product source. |
| Narrow viewer goal | The comparison must answer one ordinary, specific buyer question; it cannot use “best,” “healthiest,” “safer,” or “works better” as a blanket goal. |
| Fair alternative | The alternative must have a real, stated legitimate role before its goal-specific limitation is discussed. |
| Product fit | The hero product must address an earned gap without claiming a clinical outcome the fact record does not support. |
| Link eligibility | A single-product version may link only its exact current hero listing. Category rapid-fire linking requires a fresh Competitive Decision Map that verifies every linked TikTok Shop listing, distinct use case, and 8/10+ verdict. |

## 4. Recommended First-Test Construction — DryWater

This is a **single-product pair**, not the category rapid-fire version. It uses one familiar alternative as fair context and links only DryWater after current cart verification.

| Beat | Goal-specific content job | Evidence source |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | State the narrow choice: “If your priority is an electrolyte mix with **no added sugar** …” | Current campaign/label preflight. |
| 1 | Hold the recognizable Liquid I.V. Original package. State its legitimate role first: a conventional hydration-mix option. | Current official Liquid I.V. page/label. |
| 2 | Point to the relevant sugar line. State the limitation only for the named goal: “If no added sugar is your priority, this is not the label I would pick.” | Current exact label/FAQ. |
| 3 | Hold DryWater Raspberry Lemon and point to its exact panel. | Current exact official panel and current TikTok Shop listing. |
| 4 | Explain why it fits the stated goal: “This one has 0 grams of added sugar per stick.” | Current exact official Nutrition Facts panel. |
| 5 | Close with a single-product conditional cart CTA. | Exact current DryWater cart, seller, and offer verified on filming day. |

**Do not add:** a potassium/sodium superiority conclusion, a medical hydration claim, a “sugar is bad” label, a performance/recovery promise, a price claim, a multi-product link, or a current-offer statement without filming-day evidence.

## 5. Why the Category Rapid-Fire Version Should Wait

The multi-product “I’ll link the good ones below” version is **not yet ready** for any completed fact record. DryWater’s competitive map identifies real category alternatives, but it does not contain a current verified TikTok Shop link and earned 8/10+ decision for every potential winner. DR.DENT has thin product-video/review data and no completed current TikTok Shop competitor verification. Medicube’s alternative set contains treatments and products with different roles but lacks the fresh current product-level link/rating decisions required for co-linking.

> First test the **DryWater single pair**. Build category-specific Competitive Decision Maps only after that execution and first-party performance are recorded.
