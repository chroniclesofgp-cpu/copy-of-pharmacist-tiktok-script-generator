## What the Source Actually Establishes

The source pattern is a **false-negative purchase story**. It opens with a return, disappointment, apology, or apparent product rejection; defensively confirms the reasons the product was wanted; then reveals that the problem is not product failure but a better live deal, bundle, or price. The close turns the revealed price gap into urgency.

In the Zayava video, the creator says she is returning the product **not because** it is a 14-in-1 or because of its hair, skin, nail, bone, and wellness benefits. The reason is that she bought it by itself before the discounted bundle appeared. In the Medicube toner-pad example, the creator first confirms the individual product benefits, then says she is returning it because the brand put the bundle on discount with free shipping and a coupon.

> The source mechanism is **misdirection → reason-to-buy reminder → true price/bundle reason → urgency**, not a generic product review.

## Approved Framework Mechanics

| Beat | Job | Source-backed execution |
|---|---|---|
| **1. False negative hook** | Create immediate curiosity and expected failure. | “I’m returning this,” “I have to return this,” or pharmacist-accountability variation. The emotional register is regret, not manufactured anger. |
| **2. Authority adaptation** | Make the creator’s recommendation more consequential. | A pharmacist may adapt the setup to patient recommendations, but this is a creator-specific authority adaptation—not a verbatim source line. Use only when truthful in context. |
| **3. Three benefit defenses** | Keep the viewer watching by ruling out why the product is being returned. | Every beat must contain a concrete **feature/ingredient → plain-language benefit → viewer problem/payoff** stack. Example structure: “It’s not because it has [feature], which may help [benefit] when you are dealing with [viewer problem].” Preserve the repeated “it’s not because…” rhythm. |
| **4. Trust anchor** | Lock in the product case before the payoff. | “All of that is true and it still stands,” or an equivalent line with the same function. |
| **5. True reason / payoff** | Close the return loop. | The return is because a genuine current TikTok Shop offer, bundle, or price gap makes the prior purchase less favorable—not because the product failed. |
| **6. Urgency close** | Convert the now-resolved price tension. | Conditional cart language tied to a real current offer, sale, coupon, availability, or bundle. |

## Type Distinction That Must Be Preserved

| Asset | Runtime | Middle section | Proof behavior | Use case |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **Type 1: short apology** | 30–45 seconds | Three fast benefit defenses; no mechanism lecture. | Product visual and three-item benefit overlay stack. No PubMed study card is required because the source mechanic is speed and offer payoff. | Warm viewer; product and benefits already introduced in a prior education asset. |
| **Type 2: longer apology** | 55–75 seconds | Same three defenses, each followed by one pharmacist-only education sentence. | One properly scoped study/primary-evidence card per education line where the product category supports it. | Viewer needs the scientific “why” before the offer payoff. |

## Non-Negotiable Filming-Day Conditions

The product and benefit stack must be truthful. The offer reveal may be used only when the current cart, price, sale, bundle, shipping, coupon, or historical price statement has been checked immediately before filming. Do not claim a lowest price, specific coupon, free shipping, sold-out history, or bundle advantage that is not available or supportable that day.

## Not Established by the Source

- The exact phrase “I recommend this to all of my patients” is not source-verbatim and must not be used as generic boilerplate.
- A pharmacist must not claim actual patient returns or a personal recommendation history unless that is truthful.
- The structure does not authorize a made-up price drop, coupon, inventory warning, or bundle comparison.
- Type 1 is not the place to force a PubMed lesson. Type 2 is the evidence-forward apology variant.

## Fresh-Rebuild Use Rule

Before the next product receives this framework, reopen the live cart and source-based fact sheet, choose the Type 1 or Type 2 role, write five candidate three-line benefit stacks for user review, and select the strongest true current offer. No deleted post-trial apology script or benefit stack may be used as a model.
