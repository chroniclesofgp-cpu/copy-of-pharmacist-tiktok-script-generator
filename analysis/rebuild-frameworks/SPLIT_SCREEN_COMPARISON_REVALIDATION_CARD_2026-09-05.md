# Split-Screen Comparison — Fresh Revalidation Card

**Status:** Approved rebuilt foundation — situational use  
**Created:** September 5, 2026  
**Scope:** Two-person or clone split-screen comparison in which a complicated/worse alternative lists ingredients, products, or steps and the better-product side counters with one product that covers the relevant set.  
**Source log:** `analysis/splitscreen_comparison_hook_synthesis.md`  
**Original source set:** Four reference videos from three non-healthcare creators: @treyfindss, @deals.by.dyl, and @shopapproved.

> This card activates the format as a situational foundation. It does not claim that the non-healthcare source set proves a universal pharmacist conversion result.

---

## 1. Directly Observed Source Mechanics

| Element | Revalidated mechanic | Rebuild implication |
|---|---|---|
| Visual premise | Two sides are visible immediately: a complicated multi-product/ingredient routine versus a simpler all-in-one product. | Establish both sides from the first frame; the contrast is visual before it is verbal. |
| Interaction | The worse-alternative side names one ingredient, product, or step at a time; the better-product side counters with the same single product. | Use rapid, accurate one-to-one counters. Do not invent ingredient matches. |
| Retention | Repetition creates a “what do you have?” curiosity gap. | Delay the full product reveal until the repeated comparison has earned it. |
| Reveal | The better-product side reveals that one product contains the relevant set. | Make the reveal a confirmation of the demonstrated comparison, not a disconnected pitch. |
| Formats | The source set includes solo clone split-screen and two-person execution. | A partner or reliable clone-editing workflow is required; do not convert this into a normal monologue. |
| Close | The source formats use a partner reaction or direct purchase prompt after the reveal. | Adapt to a truthful cart CTA and current offer without inventing scarcity. |

## 2. Approved Pharmacist Adaptation

The pharmacist adaptation is approved with three constraints: the pharmacist plays the better-product side; the added education beat follows the reveal and explains one useful mechanism or formulation distinction; and every ingredient/product match must be verified against the exact product record. The adaptation may use a partner or clone edit, but it must retain the alternating comparison rhythm.

## 3. Required Beat Order

| Beat | Execution |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Text hook and both sides visible immediately. |
| 1 | Worse-alternative side names the first real ingredient, product, or step. |
| 2–4 | Better-product side counters with the same single product; repeat only for verified matches. |
| 5 | Worse-alternative side asks what the better-product side has. |
| 6 | Product reveal, followed by one concise pharmacist mechanism/benefit explanation. |
| 7 | Direct cart CTA based on current listing and offer verification. |

## 4. Eligibility Gate

Use only when the exact product genuinely contains the compared ingredients or fulfills the compared routine roles. The campaign must have a fact-only product record, a competitive decision map, and a proof-card plan for the added education beat. If the comparison requires too many forced matches, use a different framework.

## 5. Exclusions

Do not use generic alternatives, unsupported ingredient equivalence, diagnostic language, or a claim that the single product replaces every product or treatment. Do not make the pharmacist adaptation sound like a qualification disclaimer; the education must advance the product decision.
