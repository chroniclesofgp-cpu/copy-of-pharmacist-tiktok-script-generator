# Tier-List Comparison — Fresh Revalidation Card

**Status:** Approved rebuilt foundation — situational use  
**Created:** September 5, 2026  
**Scope:** A fixed letter-tier ranking of recognizable products, ingredients, formats, treatments, procedures, or alternatives, with the final linked product held for the S-tier reveal.  
**Source basis:** One preserved @flashfindstiktok0 ranking reference plus the project’s user-approved pharmacist adaptation notes and campaign-format observations.  

> This card activates the format for situational use. It does not claim broad cross-creator validation; the preserved direct source basis is one creator reference, supplemented by an approved pharmacist adaptation.

---

## 1. Directly Observed and Approved Mechanics

| Element | Revalidated mechanic | Rebuild implication |
|---|---|---|
| Opening promise | The creator ranks a recognizable category from a low tier to S-tier. | Name the category and tier system immediately; do not spend the opening explaining the grading system. |
| Prompt behavior | The off-camera prompt names the actual item first. | Never use a generic placeholder such as “S-tier product.” |
| Order | Early alternatives may jump between tiers; the final S-tier item is held for last. | Avoid a tidy C-to-B-to-A climb; preserve uncertainty while keeping the final reveal. |
| Verdict | Each item receives a tier plus a concise reason viewers understand. | Pair the tier with a role, limitation, or mechanism—not a bare letter. |
| Pharmacist adaptation | The pharmacist remains clinically neutral about legitimate alternatives and explains their limitations fairly before the final reveal. | The adaptation may distinguish efficacy, maintenance, side effects, cost, or use case without blanket attacks. |
| Final reveal | The linked product receives the final S-tier verdict and a direct mechanism/benefit explanation. | Earn the product reveal through fair alternative contrast and current product proof. |

## 2. Approved Pharmacist Adaptation Rules

The pharmacist adaptation is approved with the **clinical-neutrality rule**: acknowledge when an existing treatment or product genuinely works, then explain the limitation relevant to the stated viewer goal. The **staged-reaction rule** is also required: the off-camera person names the actual alternative or hero product, the pharmacist assigns the tier on camera, and the hero product is not introduced through a generic placeholder.

## 3. Required Beat Order

| Beat | Execution |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Tier-list title and category appear immediately. |
| 1–3 | Off-camera prompts name recognizable alternatives; pharmacist assigns deliberately nonsequential tiers with one direct reason each. |
| 4 | Final prompt names the actual hero product or product architecture. |
| 5 | Pharmacist assigns S-tier, explains the mechanism/benefit, and shows the exact product. |
| 6 | Current cart CTA follows only after product, proof, and offer verification. |

## 4. Eligibility Gate

Use only with a current Competitive Decision Map containing recognizable alternatives and fair, source-supported limitations. The product must genuinely earn the final position for the stated goal. More than one option may earn a high tier when distinct use cases justify it; do not force every alternative into a low tier.

## 5. Evidence and Compliance Boundaries

Use proof cards for ingredient or clinical claims and distinguish ingredient evidence from exact-product evidence. Keep sentence-level qualification where needed, but do not remove the useful mechanism or viewer-facing benefit. Do not make unsupported treatment, cure, superiority, or universal-result claims.

## 6. Validation Boundary

The current direct source basis is one @flashfindstiktok0 reference. The framework is therefore **situationally approved**, not broadly cross-creator validated. Future creator audits may strengthen the evidence count, but they must not be represented as completed until exact source videos are reviewed.
