# Proposed Proven CTA Bank — Urgency-First Revision

**Purpose:** Give every linked TikTok Shop script a preselected, proven CTA that creates a real reason to act now. The CTA is not an afterthought: it is the final conversion mechanism after the video has earned the viewer’s trust.

**Status:** Proposed only. This document does **not** yet change `PHRASE_BANK.md`, script defaults, or permanent project rules.

---

## Core Decision

> **Every linked product script should end in urgency.** The default is not a soft “here is the link” close. It is a conditional-cart, historical-sellout, live-sale, or live-bundle close that tells the viewer why waiting may cost them the current opportunity.

The creator does **not** need real-time inventory counts to create legitimate FOMO. The CTA should rely on the visible cart, the product’s established demand, a documented historical sellout pattern, or a promotion/bundle visible when filming. It must never create medical urgency or claim a precise stock count that the creator cannot see.

A CTA is always the **last spoken section**. It ends with a visible physical downward point.

## CTA Bank A — Default Conditional-Cart FOMO

Use these for the majority of linked MOF/BOF videos. They create an action deadline without pretending to know the live unit count.

| ID | CTA line | Source status | Best fit | Required delivery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **U-01** | “If you still see the orange cart below, I would grab it before it sells out again.” | Proven creator structure; product-specific adaptation | Most viral / high-demand products | Look at the cart area and point down once on “below” |
| **U-02** | “If you want to check it out, I’ll leave it in the orange link down below. But if you can’t see it anymore, that means they’re all gone.” | @mercer_house, verbatim | Any linked TikTok Shop product | Direct eye contact, then one downward point |
| **U-03** | “If you want to grab this, I’m going to leave the link below—just don’t wait too long if the cart is still there.” | rphreviews structure + urgency adaptation | TOF/MOF product reveal | Hold product, then point down only on final clause |
| **U-04** | “The one I recommend is by [BRAND]. If you still see it in the orange cart below, I’d grab it.” | naturo + rphreviews structure | Expert verdict / pharmacist-recommendation video | Product in hand; one final downward point |

### Default eligibility

`U-01` through `U-04` are eligible for established, actively sold products with a correctly linked cart. They rely on the observable **cart availability** rather than an unverified same-day stock count.

## CTA Bank B — Live Sale, Bundle, and Price-Opportunity FOMO

Use these when the promotion or bundle is visibly active on the listing when filming. Do not cite an exact price unless it is verified and intentionally shown on screen.

| ID | CTA line | Source status | Best fit | Filming-day check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P-01** | “It’s on sale right now, so if you still see that orange cart below, I would grab one while the offer is still live.” | Proven affiliate sale structure | Current sale | Confirm active sale badge / listing text before filming |
| **P-02** | “If you still see this bundle in the orange cart, I would take advantage while they’re still offering it together.” | Bundle-value adaptation | Official bundle / duo / set | Confirm exact bundle and components |
| **P-03** | “If you want to grab this deal before it runs out, click the orange card below.” | rphreviews, verbatim | Deal / bundle BOF | Confirm live offer and correct cart |
| **P-04** | “This is one of those products where the price and bundles change, so if you still see the offer below, I would not wait on it.” | Affiliate FOMO structure; non-price-specific adaptation | Price-sensitive / bundle content | Confirm there is a live offer, but no need to predict a specific future price |

### Default eligibility

These lines may say **“on sale,” “offer,” “deal,”** or **“bundle”** only when that fact is visible on the linked listing when filmed. They do not require a known end date. They should not say a price “will go up tomorrow” or that a discount is “ending tonight” without that specific platform evidence.

## Non-Negotiable Script Workflow

1. **Choose a CTA ID before drafting the final section.** Every script header records `CTA ID`, `urgency trigger`, and `filming-day check`.
2. **Use FOMO by default.** A linked MOF/BOF script should not end with a neutral “link below” unless no defensible urgency trigger exists.
3. **Match the urgency type to evidence.** Use `U` for cart availability/high demand, `P` for a visible sale/bundle, `S` for documented historical sellout or live restock, and `C` for comparisons.
4. **Do not stack urgency stories.** One strong reason to act is more credible than “sale + only a few left + restock + buy now.”
5. **Do not create health urgency.** The CTA urgency is about the shopping opportunity, never a claim that delaying the product harms the viewer’s health.
6. **End physically.** The final words coincide with one clear downward point. Nothing follows.
7. **Log the CTA ID.** Record it alongside clicks, conversion, saves, and watch data so this creator’s own numbers determine which urgency mechanism wins.

---

## Do Not Use

| Excluded line / behavior | Why |
|---|---|
| “They got a couple left.” | Claims a precise inventory state the creator cannot verify. |
| “It’s ending tonight” / “price will go up tomorrow.” | Requires a specific deadline or future-price proof. |
| “They’re giving it away for damn near free.” | Price framing is unprofessional and may be inaccurate. |
| “This will sell out because it is so good.” | Turns an inferred outcome into a guarantee. |
| A feature, study, or caveat after the CTA starts | The CTA must be final and the downward point must land cleanly. |
