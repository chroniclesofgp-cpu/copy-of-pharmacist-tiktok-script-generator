# Master Context vs. Six Full Script-Writing Documents

**Audit date:** September 5, 2026  
**Scope:** Identify active script-writing rules, protocols, gates, and workflow controls documented outside the six full writing references.

## Six-doc baseline

The Master Context identifies these as the six full script-writing sources:

| # | Document | Primary role |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `HOOK_FRAMEWORKS.md` | Proven hook structures, openings, section sequences, creator attribution, and framework-specific mechanics |
| 2 | `SCRIPT_ARCHITECTURE_GUIDE.md` | Universal structural rules, timing, authority, education, CTA, proof, and objection architecture |
| 3 | `BUYER_PSYCHOLOGY_LEVERS.md` | Conversion levers, sequencing, trust stack, desire amplification, and hook/lever combinations |
| 4 | `PHRASE_BANK.md` | Reusable transition, gap, objection, rehook, and CTA language, including CTA IDs |
| 5 | `VISUAL_OVERLAY_PLAYBOOK.md` | Timed overlay strategies, proof-card behavior, visual interruptions, product shots, and CTA arrows |
| 6 | `HEALTHCARE_HOOK_REFERENCE_GUIDE.md` | Human-readable execution distinctions and selected creator techniques not fully repeated in the framework library |

`PRE_SESSION_BRIEF.md` is a compressed substitute for these six documents during short sessions; it is not a seventh full source document.

## Executive conclusion

Yes. The Master Context and several external protocol documents contain important controls that do not appear as complete, standalone rules in the six-doc writing stack. The most consequential are **workflow gates**, **source and product-intelligence prerequisites**, **the authoritative post-write checklist**, **TikTok Shop health/beauty preflight**, **clean-slate source boundaries**, **handle verification**, and **BOF-specific templates**.

The six docs mainly explain **how a script should be structured and made persuasive**. The documents outside the six determine **when scripting is allowed to begin, what evidence must be loaded, what must be checked before delivery or filming, how scripts are stored, and which special formats have separate templates**.

## The separate post-write checklist adds active writing gates

`POST_WRITE_CHECKLIST.md` is the clearest document containing rules that are not fully present in the six-doc stack. Its current controls include:

| Checklist control | Practical requirement |
|---|---|
| Item 0 — Hook Framework Validation | Name the framework, quote the spoken hook, confirm the proven opening pattern, and reject label/announcement hooks. Expert-verdict openings must make the specific bold claim, not announce that a verdict is coming. |
| CTA ID and filming-day verification | Select a CTA ID from `PHRASE_BANK.md`; verify the corresponding live cart, sale, restock, or comparison condition immediately before filming. The downward point must be the final action. |
| Distinct product proof | Provide at least two distinct, source-verified reasons to choose the exact product, each mapped to a buyer payoff and proof source. Repeated dosage or safety notes do not count twice. |
| Visual interruption cadence | Plan a new visual element approximately every 3–5 seconds, not only at the opening and close. |
| Transition bridges | Add a verbal bridge at each major section transition. |
| Specific safety note | Prefer a named interaction, drug, condition, or population over a generic disclaimer when the product has a known issue. |
| Empathetic alternative | If a safety instruction creates a purchase barrier, provide a realistic alternative that preserves the safety message. |
| Rule E — no sentence redundancy | Every line must add new information or advance the argument. The audit must quote failed lines rather than merely checking a box. |
| Product-advancement/package-repetition pass | Assign every beat one job and one new fact/payoff; scan the whole campaign for repeated credential phrases, safety notes, comparator caveats, or product facts. |
| Rule F — pain-first mechanism | Lead with the viewer’s lived problem, then explain the mechanism as the answer. Do not open with a clinical term followed by an abstract lecture. |
| Rule 11 — viewer-intelligible language | Replace unexplained clinical jargon with plain English or a familiar analogy. |
| Colloquial pain language | Prefer phrases viewers use about themselves, such as “turkey neck,” over clinical labels such as “skin laxity.” |
| Motivating close | Do not end transformation content on a timeline hedge; close on aspiration, commitment, or an immediate attainable win. |
| Clickable study links | Every study reference in the script file must be a clickable PubMed/PMC Markdown link. |
| Funnel calibration | Check that hook, education, product-reveal timing, and CTA match the declared TOF/MOF/BOF stage. |
| Verdict-first expert verdict | For warm MOF→BOF expert-verdict scripts, lead with the verdict and use the credential as the reason, not as the opening. |

## TikTok Shop health/beauty preflight adds filming and claim controls

`TIKTOK_SHOP_HEALTH_BEAUTY_COMPLIANCE_PREFLIGHT.md` is not one of the six writing references, but it controls linked health, supplement, and beauty content over older architecture examples. Its requirements include:

1. Build an exact current PDP/package claim map for every product-level claim, ingredient function, timing, certification, origin, and quantity.
2. Do not diagnose, imply a condition, recommend medication substitution, or present a linked product as treating, preventing, or managing a condition.
3. Do not convert an ingredient mechanism or study into an unsupported result for the linked SKU.
4. Separate a factual credential from an endorsement implication; a white coat is not by itself an automatic trigger.
5. Use actual product, label, official PDP, and neutral routine visuals; do not use condition imagery, transformation imagery, altered visuals, or study screenshots as apparent linked-product proof.
6. Verify live price, offer, stock, review, and urgency conditions immediately before filming. A disclaimer does not cure an unsupported claim.
7. Use the exact supported product wording rather than weakening a verified benefit into empty language such as “tone care.”
8. Add the required preflight footer to the script or filming package with the claim map, removed product-level claims, credential framing, visual status, and filming approval.

## BOF-specific rules outside the six

`BOF_INSTRUCTION_CORRECTION_TEMPLATES.md` is required for any BOF suite session. It adds:

- A three-script suite: Type 1 short apology, Type 2 longer “It’s Not Because,” and Type 3 instruction-correction.
- Type 1 and Type 2 should be A/B tested; Type 3 can run after the first TOF video.
- Type 1 uses pain points and desired outcomes, not mechanism education; Type 2 adds one pharmacist education sentence per benefit; Type 3 fills the how-to-use gap.
- Every Type 1/2 script requires five fully written benefit-stack options, with Option 1 as the default.
- Type 1’s reveal must close the curiosity loop: the product is being returned because of price, not product failure.
- Type 4 scam-warning uses separate Path A/Path B rules; literal “fake/scam” language requires preserved current counterfeit or seller-mismatch evidence, otherwise use the criteria-led path.

## Documentation gaps and conflicts found

The Master Context itself contains a few documentation-maintenance issues that should be kept in mind:

1. The document header says it was last updated August 17, 2026, while later sections document July 27, August, and September clean-slate work. The operational sections are newer than the header and should not be treated as a reliable freshness indicator.
2. The Master Context says every script must be audited against the 18-point brief checklist, but Section 19e states that `POST_WRITE_CHECKLIST.md` replaced that condensed list and is authoritative with 21 items. The current rule should be: use the standalone 21-item checklist; the brief is only a compressed pointer.
3. `SCRIPT_ARCHITECTURE_GUIDE.md` contains a dated August compliance override, while the dedicated TikTok Shop preflight is the more focused control for linked health/beauty content. The preflight should govern when the two differ.
4. The Master Context’s older Section 3 hook tables are explicitly stale. `HOOK_FRAMEWORKS.md` and the rebuilt source map are authoritative for current hook selection.
5. The six-doc stack and the operational protocols are complementary, not interchangeable. Reading the six docs alone is insufficient for a new product or a linked health/beauty script because it does not establish exact-product evidence, live-offer verification, source reload behavior, or final post-write/filming approval.

## Bottom line

The answer is **yes**: important script-writing controls exist outside the six main writing documents. The most important missing layer is not another creative framework; it is the **operating control system around writing**: exact-product fact verification, campaign approval, source loading, clean-slate provenance, post-write itemized auditing, handle verification, live CTA/offer checks, and TikTok Shop health/beauty preflight. Those controls should be treated as mandatory gates around the six creative references rather than merged into the six documents indiscriminately.
