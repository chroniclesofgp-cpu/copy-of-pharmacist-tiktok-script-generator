## SECTION 1 — UNIVERSAL OVERLAY RULES (confirmed 5/5 creators)

These patterns appear in ALL five creators. They are non-negotiable structural elements.

### Rule 1: Credential badge/overlay from frame 0
Every creator establishes a visual credential in the first 3 seconds — before speaking or alongside the first spoken word. The form varies by creator but the function is identical.

| Creator | Form |
|---|---|
| rphreviews | Red text banner with "You MUST watch this video ⚠️" + throwback pharmacy photo at credential delivery |
| Drew | Lab coat + ID badge visible from frame 0 |
| Riva | "Riva Pharmacist" or "Licensed Pharmacist" text overlay + pharmacy license popup (2–3 seconds) |
| Dr. Faith | "Doctor Explains" red badge overlay in first 3 seconds |
| Naturo | "From A Certified Naturopath" text badge from frame 0 |

**Verified finding:** The credential is always visual, not just verbal. The viewer sees authority before they hear it.

---

### Rule 2: PubMed study screenshots as visual proof
All 5 creators show PubMed or journal article screenshots during the mechanism/education section. Frequency varies:

| Creator | Study Screenshots Per Video | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| rphreviews | 1–3 per video (11/23 videos) | Mechanism section, 2–3 seconds each |
| Drew | 8–15 per video (20/20 videos) | Mechanism section, rapid-fire |
| Riva | 1–3 per video (15/22 videos) | Mechanism section |
| Dr. Faith | 3–5 per video (9/10 videos) | Mechanism section |
| Naturo | 6–10 per video (9/9 videos) | Mechanism section, rapid-fire |

**Verified finding:** Study screenshots are shown for 2–3 seconds each — long enough to register as "real research" but not long enough for the viewer to read the full title. The visual presence of the study is the conversion element, not the study content.

---

### Rule 3: Physical product hold-up at reveal
All 5 creators physically bring the product into frame at the moment of reveal. No creator uses a static product image or cut to a product photo.

**Verified finding:** The product is always physically handled — picked up, held next to the face, or held up with both hands. This signals authenticity and creates a moment of visual focus.

---

### Rule 4: Education occupies 55–75% of runtime
All 5 creators spend the majority of the video on education before the product reveal.

| Creator | Education % of Runtime |
|---|---|
| rphreviews | 55–70% |
| Drew | 60–75% |
| Riva | 55–70% |
| Dr. Faith | 58–75% |
| Naturo | 60–75% |

---

### Rule 5: Product reveal at 55–88% of runtime
All 5 creators reveal the product in the second half of the video.

---

### Rule 6: Physical downward-pointing gesture at CTA
All 5 creators physically point their finger downward toward where the TikTok Shop link appears at the moment of the CTA.

---

### Rule 7: Stock-based scarcity in CTA
All 5 creators use stock-based scarcity language ("before it runs out," "if you still see it in stock," "snag it while you can"). Time-based scarcity (countdown timers) is not used by any creator.

---

### Rule 8: Supply duration framing — never price
All 5 creators communicate value through supply duration ("4-month supply," "300 capsules") and savings framing ("exclusive bundle," "great deals right now"). Price is never stated.

---

### Rule 9: "No fillers / third-party tested / made in USA" proof anchor
All 5 creators use a near-identical quality proof phrase just after the product reveal.

---

### Rule 10: Proactive objection handling (2–4 per video)
All 5 creators address objections before they form, not after. Always placed in the education section or immediately after the product reveal.

## SECTION 2 — CREATOR-SPECIFIC OVERLAY TECHNIQUES

### rphreviews
**Opening red text hook (17/23 videos):**
- Format: Red pill-shaped or rectangular text box
- Text: "[Conditional qualifier] + [Specific benefit or problem] + You MUST watch this video ⚠️"
- Duration: **3–8 seconds** — then disappears
- Placement: Top of screen, full width or near-full width
- Color: Red background, white bold text

**Checkmark stack (18/23 videos):**
- Format: Rapid sequence of benefit overlays with ✅ symbols
- Duration: 8–15 seconds total, each benefit appears for 1–2 seconds and stacks
- Placement: Product reveal section — after product is shown, before social proof
- Covers 4–8 benefits
- Creates visual rhythm that signals "this product does everything"

**Throwback pharmacy photo (19/23 videos):**
- Format: Photo of him younger in a pharmacist coat with name tag, in a retail pharmacy
- Duration: 1–2 seconds
- Placement: During verbal credential delivery ("I've been a pharmacist for over 17 years")
- Function: Proves credential predates TikTok — implies no financial motivation to fabricate it

**CTA arrow overlay (20/23 videos):**
- Format: Large colored arrow (pink, yellow, orange, or red) pointing downward
- Animated (bouncing/pulsing) in 8/23 videos
- Appears simultaneously with verbal CTA
- Combined with physical finger-pointing gesture

**Study screenshots (11/23 videos):**
- Shown for 2–3 seconds each
- Placed in mechanism section only
- Never in hook or CTA

**TikTok Shop screenshot overlay (9/23 videos):**
- Shows product listing with star rating, review count, and units sold
- Placed in pre-CTA section
- Shown visually AND stated verbally simultaneously

**Quality certification cluster (14/23 videos):**
- Three badges shown simultaneously: GMP Certified + Third Party Tested + Made in USA
- Placed in pre-CTA section
- Visual cluster, no verbal explanation needed

## SECTION 3 — WHAT IS NOT A PERSISTENT BANNER

**Confirmed finding across all 5 creators:** No creator in this dataset uses a text overlay that stays on screen for the entire video. The "persistent banner" concept as previously described in this playbook does not exist in any of the 84 analyzed videos.

What creators DO use:
- **Opening hook text** (3–8 seconds) — then disappears
- **Credential badge** (3–5 seconds) — then disappears
- **Checkmark stacks** (1–2 seconds per item, stacking during product reveal section)
- **Study screenshots** (2–3 seconds each, mechanism section)
- **Review popups** (1–2 seconds each, post-reveal section)
- **CTA arrow** (final 5–15 seconds only)

The only "persistent" visual element is the **physical environment and costume** — lab coat, credential badge clipped to coat, bookshelf background, products on desk. These are always-on authority signals that require no overlay.

## SECTION 5 — WHAT YOUR PHYSICAL SETUP REPLACES

For a creator with a strong physical setup (diplomas on wall, pharmacy textbooks, mortar and pestle, white coat, products on desk), the following overlays are partially or fully replaced by the environment:

| Overlay | Replaced By |
|---|---|
| Credential badge text | Physical diplomas on wall (always-on, no overlay needed) |
| "Doctor Explains" red badge | White coat + physical credentials visible |
| Throwback pharmacy photo | Diplomas prove credential predates TikTok |
| Quality certification cluster | Can be stated verbally — physical lab setting implies quality |

**Implication:** A creator with a strong physical credential environment (like naturo's bookshelf setup or your diploma wall) needs fewer text overlays than a creator filming in a plain setting (like rphreviews in a pharmacy aisle). The physical environment does the work that overlays do for other creators.
