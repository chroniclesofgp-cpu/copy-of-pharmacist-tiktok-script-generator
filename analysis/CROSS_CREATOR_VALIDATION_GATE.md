## NEW HOOK FRAMEWORK CANDIDATES FROM DR. FAITH + NATURO

### Candidate 1: "Side Effect" Surprise Hook (naturo Rank 1 — $216K GMV)
**What it is:** Opens with an unexpected, desirable *side effect* or *bonus outcome* rather than the intended use. The viewer is placed in a scenario where they already have the product and are surprised by a benefit they didn't expect.

**Verbatim example:** "So you took astaxanthin for back pain and suddenly your skin is glowing and tan like it's still the middle of summer? As a certified naturopath, I've seen so many people pleasantly surprised by the sun-kissed glow they get from astaxanthin."

**Check against existing frameworks:**
- Storytime Hook (Framework 12): Personal story, creator is protagonist. This is NOT personal — it's second-person ("you took..."). Different structure.
- Symptom Checklist (Framework 2): Opens with problems. This opens with a positive surprise. Different emotional tone.
- After 1 Month (Framework 4): Results hook — shows outcomes after use. This is structurally similar but the key difference is the *surprise/unexpected* framing. The viewer didn't expect this outcome — that's the hook. The After 1 Month hook is about expected results. The Side Effect hook is about unexpected bonus results.

**Verdict: NEW FRAMEWORK.** The "unexpected bonus outcome" structure is distinct. It works because it creates a curiosity gap for both existing users (who want to understand the side effect) and non-users (who want the side effect). The positive surprise framing is the opposite of the fear-based hooks — it is the only hook in the library that leads with a pleasant surprise rather than a problem or question.

**Cross-creator validation:** Only confirmed in 1 creator (naturo). Does not meet the 3/5 threshold for a universal rule. However, it is naturo's highest-GMV video ($216K) and the format is structurally distinct. Adding as **Tier 2 — Situational** with a note that it is high-ceiling but limited data.

### Candidate 2: "Anti-Gatekeeping" Persona Hook (Dr. Faith — multiple videos)
**What it is:** Opens with or includes the phrase "I don't gatekeep" as a trust accelerant. Positions the creator as an insider sharing information that others withhold.

**Check against existing frameworks:**
- Suppressed Knowledge Hook (Framework 3): "Here's what they don't want you to know." The anti-gatekeeping hook is similar but the framing is personal rather than conspiratorial. "I don't gatekeep" is about the creator's character; "they don't want you to know" is about an external suppressor.

**Verdict: NOT a new framework.** This is a creator-specific execution style for the Suppressed Knowledge Hook. The "I don't gatekeep" phrase is Dr. Faith's personal brand language — it is not a replicable structural format. Adding as an execution note to Framework 3 (Suppressed Knowledge).

### Candidate 3: "Bait and Switch Trust Architecture" (Dr. Faith — 6/10 videos)
**What it is:** Introduces the specific product early (0:07–0:15) but immediately backs off, saying "I'll help you regardless of which brand you bought." Delivers 60–90 seconds of brand-agnostic education. Returns to the specific product for the close.

**Check against existing frameworks:**
- Right-Way Hook (Framework 23/24): Similar structure — education before pitch. But the Bait and Switch explicitly names the product early, then withdraws it, which is structurally different from simply delaying the product reveal.
- Instruction-Correction Hook (Framework 1): Education-heavy, product late. But doesn't include the explicit "I'll help you regardless of brand" withdrawal.

**Verdict: NOT a new hook framework.** This is a mid-video execution technique, not an opening hook. The hook (the first 3 seconds) is still a Right-Way or Expert Verdict hook. The Bait and Switch is what happens in the middle of the video. Adding as an execution note to SCRIPT_ARCHITECTURE_GUIDE.md.

### Candidate 4: "Granular Dosage as Conversion Driver" (Dr. Faith — 6/10 videos)
**What it is:** Gives exact dosage instructions by age bracket (Under 35: X mg, Over 35: Y mg, 60+: Z mg), timing relative to meals, and specific interactions to avoid. Makes the video feel like a genuine medical consultation.

**Verdict: NOT a new hook framework.** This is a mid-video education technique. Adding as an execution note to SCRIPT_ARCHITECTURE_GUIDE.md.
