## NEW FRAMEWORK CANDIDATES

### Candidate A — Stitch/News-Reaction Authority
**Evidence:** Rank 01 ($97K — his highest GMV video)  
**Structure:** Open with a news clip or viral video establishing a health problem → Enter as the authority who already knew this was coming ("I didn't have to see the news to know what's going on") → Mechanism/education → Product as solution  
**Psych trigger:** Social proof (news validates the problem) + authority (pharmacist already knew) + urgency (it's happening now)  
**Replicability:** High — any relevant health news clip or viral health video can be the setup. The creator does not need to be tagged or collaborate with the source.  
**Recommendation:** Elevate to new framework. This is rph's highest-GMV video and the format is directly replicable.

### Candidate B — Comment-Reply/Q&A Authority
**Evidence:** Ranks 16 ($19,872) and 27 ($7,059) — two confirmed examples  
**Structure:** Show a viewer comment on screen → Read the comment aloud → Authority → Answer the question as a pharmacist consultation → Product recommendation if applicable  
**Psych trigger:** Social proof (real viewer question) + parasocial trust (pharmacist answering your question) + FOMO (other viewers are getting this advice)  
**Replicability:** High — can be done with any real comment or a staged comment. rph uses real comments.  
**Recommendation:** Elevate to new framework. Two confirmed examples in top 30, and this format appears extensively in his profile grid (7 of 34 videos in the original analysis).

### Candidate C — Generation Humor Hook
**Evidence:** Rank 24 ($7,647) — one example  
**Structure:** Humor hook referencing age/generation → Authority embedded in the joke → Product introduction early → Mechanism education → Playful CTA  
**Psych trigger:** Relatability + humor + authority (embedded, not stated)  
**Replicability:** Moderate — requires the right product and the right humor angle. Not applicable to all products.  
**Recommendation:** Note as a variant, not a standalone framework. Insufficient evidence from one video. Watch for additional examples in other creators.
