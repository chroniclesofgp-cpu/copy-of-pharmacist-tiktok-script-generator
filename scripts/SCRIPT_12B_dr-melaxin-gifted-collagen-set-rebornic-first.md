## Filming Notes

- **Lead with the full set box, then immediately pick up the pink serum.** The box establishes this is a set video. The pink serum creates the visual question that the hook answers.
- **The REBORNIC mechanism is the star.** Slow down slightly on "microscopic needle-like structures" and "micro-channels" — these are the words that make the viewer stop and think "wait, what?" Let that land before moving on.
- **The Multi Balm is the value-stack anchor.** When you name it, hold it up and let the viewer see it. Say "bestselling" with conviction — it is a fact and it signals that this product has been validated by purchase behavior, not just reviews.
- **The B12 reveal is the mid-video rehook.** Deliver "and here is where it gets interesting" with a slight pause before it — this is the moment that re-engages viewers who are at the scroll-off risk point (~30 seconds in).
- **Spicule sensation note:** If you have used the serum, briefly mentioning the mild tingling on application adds authenticity — frame it as "you will feel it working" not "it might sting." This pre-empts the objection and turns a potential negative into a proof point.
- **Do not rush the value stack section.** Each product sentence is a mini-sale. Give each one a beat. The viewer is mentally calculating whether the set is worth it — the value stack is where you win or lose that calculation.

## A/B Test Notes

**Version A (B12-first):** Opens with the pink color as the curiosity hook. Best for: TOF audiences who have never heard of Dr. Melaxin. The B12 angle is uniquely pharmacist-owned and has zero creator competition.

**Version B (REBORNIC-first — this script):** Opens with the delivery technology as the curiosity hook. Best for: audiences who have seen collagen content before and are skeptical. The "why other serums don't work" angle speaks directly to the "I've tried collagen and didn't see results" objection. Also stronger for selling the set as a whole, because REBORNIC is the thread connecting all 5 products.

**What to watch in the data:** If Version A outperforms on watch time but Version B outperforms on conversion, the B12 hook is better for discovery and the REBORNIC hook is better for purchase intent. Consider using Version A as the TOF video and Version B as the MOF retargeting video.
