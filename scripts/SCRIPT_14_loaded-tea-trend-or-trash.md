## FILMING NOTES

- **Total estimated runtime:** 65–75 seconds at a natural pharmacist pace
- **Opening visual is critical:** The colorful drink on camera in the first frame is what stops the scroll before the verbal hook lands. Make it vibrant.
- **Pause after the verbal hook:** Let "coming from a pharmacist" land for 1 beat before the tear-down starts. This is Riva's signature pacing technique.
- **Dynamic captions throughout:** Every sentence should have a caption. This is not optional for this hook type.
- **Ingredient text overlays:** Put each key ingredient name on screen as you say it — "L-theanine," "B12," "Menaquinone-7." These are the scroll-stopping moments in the middle of the video.
- **Study pop-up placement:** The L-theanine/caffeine study pop-up goes on screen during the L-theanine explanation. The B-vitamin/mitochondria study pop-up goes on screen during the B-vitamin section. Keep each one on screen for 2–3 seconds.
