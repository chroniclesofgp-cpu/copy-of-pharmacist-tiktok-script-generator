**[EDUCATION — The Mechanism]**
Kojic acid works by blocking an enzyme called tyrosinase — the enzyme your skin uses to convert tyrosine into melanin. No tyrosinase activity, less melanin gets produced. That is a fundamentally different mechanism than most brightening ingredients, which work downstream, trying to stop melanin after it is already being made.
