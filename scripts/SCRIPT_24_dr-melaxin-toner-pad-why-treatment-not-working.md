## Filming Notes

- **This video targets frustrated buyers.** The tone should be empathetic, not critical. You are not saying "you did it wrong" — you are saying "here is the missing piece, and it is not your fault nobody told you."

- **The "dead skin cells" visual explanation is the key moment.** If you can physically demonstrate — even just describing the concept with your hands — it makes the mechanism concrete and memorable.

- **The product reveal is the solution, not the pitch.** Frame it as "this is the product that solves both problems" — not "buy this product." The instruction-correction hook works because the product is the logical answer to the problem you just explained.

- **Keep the safety note brief.** This video is already MOF — the audience is warm and does not need as much education as a TOF video. The safety note is a one-liner, not a full explanation.

- **Best deployment:** Post this as the third or fourth video in the toner pad sequence, after the Glycolic Acid Warning (Script 21) and the 5-Pathway Breakdown (Script 22) have built awareness. This video converts warm viewers who are already interested but have not pulled the trigger.
