## SCRIPT

**[HOOK — 0:00–0:07]**
So they shoved this Medicube deodorant down your throat and told you it was going to fix your underarms — but then they didn't tell you how to use it. Well, I'm a pharmacist, and I'm going to fix that.

**[AUTHORITY — 0:07–0:13]**
I've been counseling patients on skincare for over a decade. And the number one question I get after someone buys this is: when do I apply it, and how long before I actually see results?

**[ANSWER — TIMING — 0:13–0:30]**
The best time to apply this is at night — right before bed. Here is why: kojic acid, the brightening ingredient in this formula, works by blocking the enzyme that produces melanin. That process is happening at the cellular level, and it needs time to work without being washed off. If you apply it in the morning, you are sweating it off, washing it off, and rubbing it off on your clothes before it has had a chance to do anything. Night application means it is sitting on your skin for 6 to 8 hours uninterrupted. That is when the brightening actually happens.

**[ANSWER — RESULTS TIMELINE — 0:30–0:47]**
Now the results question — and this is the part the viral videos are not being honest about. The before-and-afters you are seeing in a week or two — those are not what most people experience. Kojic acid works by blocking the enzyme that produces melanin. That is a biological process. It runs on your skin's own cycle. The clinical data on kojic acid shows that meaningful brightening takes a minimum of six to eight weeks of consistent nightly use. Some people see initial changes at four weeks. But if you stopped using this at two weeks because you didn't see anything — that is why it didn't work. You stopped before the biology had time to catch up.

**[PHARMACIST FLAG — 0:47–0:57]**
One thing to note that commonly gets missed: if you are using a retinol or a glycolic acid product anywhere near your underarms — or if you are using a separate brightening serum on your body — do not layer it with this. Kojic acid is already doing the brightening work. Stacking multiple actives on the same area can cause irritation, especially on skin that is already sensitized from shaving. One active at a time.

**[PRODUCT REVEAL — 0:57–1:10]**
The one I recommend is this Medicube Body Brightening Deodorant. Apply it at night after showering. Two to three swipes is enough — you do not need to overload it. Give it six to eight weeks for visible brightening — and up to twelve weeks for the full result. And because there is no aluminum and no AHA, it is safe for daily use without cycling.

**[CTA — 1:10–1:18]**
I'll leave the link in the orange cart below. If you have questions about layering it with other skincare or how long results are taking for you, drop them in the comments — I read them. *(point down)*
