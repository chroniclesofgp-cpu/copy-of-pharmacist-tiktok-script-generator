## SCRIPT

**[HOOK — 0:00–0:07]**
If you're struggling to fall asleep or stay asleep — there is a specific form of magnesium that has a clinical trial behind it. And most people are not taking it.

**[AUTHORITY — 0:07–0:15]**
I'm a pharmacist. I've been counseling patients on sleep supplements for over a decade. And when someone comes to me asking about magnesium for sleep, the first thing I ask is: which form are you taking?

**[PROBLEM — 0:15–0:28]**
Because here's what nobody tells you — magnesium oxide, which is the most common form in cheap supplements, has one of the lowest absorption rates of any form. It passes through your system mostly unabsorbed. So if you're taking magnesium and still waking up at 3am, the form is the problem, not the mineral.

**[REHOOK — 0:28–0:33]**
And here's the part that actually surprised me when I looked at the research.

**[MECHANISM — 0:33–0:65]**
There is a published randomized controlled trial — the Abbasi study, 2012 — that looked specifically at magnesium supplementation for insomnia. The finding: magnesium significantly improved sleep efficiency, total sleep time, and early morning awakening. The form used in that study was magnesium glycinate. Here's why that form works for sleep specifically — glycinate is bound to the amino acid glycine. Glycine on its own has calming, sleep-promoting properties. It works on NMDA receptors in the brain to reduce core body temperature, which is one of the signals your body uses to initiate sleep. So you're getting the magnesium benefit and the glycine benefit simultaneously. That's a mechanism no other form of magnesium delivers.

**[GAP — 1:05–1:15]**
The problem is that most magnesium supplements either don't include glycinate at all, or they include it in such small amounts that you're not getting a therapeutic dose. You're paying for a sleep supplement that doesn't have the form the research was actually done on.

**[PRODUCT REVEAL — 1:15–1:30]**
The one I recommend is the Toplux Magnesium Complex. It includes magnesium glycinate as one of 8 forms — so you're getting the sleep form plus seven others that cover energy, heart health, and recovery. It's non-GMO, vegan, and the capsule form means better absorption than tablets.

**[BRAND CLOSE + OBJECTION HANDLE — 1:30–1:45]**
One caveat — if you have kidney disease, do not supplement magnesium without talking to your doctor first. Magnesium is cleared by the kidneys, and supplementing with impaired kidney function can cause toxicity. Also, take this at night — magnesium glycinate works best when taken 30–60 minutes before bed. That's when the glycine mechanism is most effective.
