## FILMING NOTES

- **Total estimated runtime:** 80–90 seconds at a natural pharmacist pace
- **Opening visual is critical:** Both products in frame simultaneously. This is the comparison hook's most important visual moment — it signals "I'm going to be fair" before a word is spoken.
- **Celsius section tone:** Genuinely fair. Do not dismiss Celsius. The six university studies are real and worth mentioning — it makes your eventual Loaded Tea recommendation more credible, not less.
- **Pivot line pacing:** Pause after "transparency is part of what I look for as a pharmacist." This is the turning point of the video. Give it a full beat.
- **Study pop-up placement:** The L-theanine/caffeine study pop-up goes on screen during the L-theanine explanation in the Loaded Tea section.
- **Proprietary blend line:** This is the clinical insight that makes the comparison feel like expert advice. Deliver it matter-of-factly, not as an attack.
- **Conclusion tone:** "Both are good products" is essential. This is what separates a pharmacist recommendation from a sales pitch. Say it like you mean it.
