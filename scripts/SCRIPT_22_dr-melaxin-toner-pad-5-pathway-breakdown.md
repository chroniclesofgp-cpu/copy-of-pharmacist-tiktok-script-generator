**[EDUCATION — The 5 Pathways]**
Dark spots form through a multi-step process. Melanin gets triggered, produced, transferred to skin cells, and then sits there. Most products only interrupt one step. Here is what this pad is doing at all five.

**Pathway one — Kojic Acid.**
Kojic acid is a fermentation byproduct — it is found in sake and soy sauce production. It works by blocking tyrosinase, the enzyme your skin needs to produce melanin in the first place. No enzyme activity, less melanin gets made.

*[On-screen overlay: "1. KOJIC ACID → Blocks Tyrosinase → Less Melanin Produced"]*

**Pathway two — Tranexamic Acid.**
Tranexamic acid was originally a prescription drug used to stop bleeding. Researchers discovered it also interrupts the UV signaling pathway that tells your skin to produce more melanin after sun exposure. It attacks the signal, not the enzyme — a completely different mechanism than kojic acid.

*[On-screen overlay: "2. TRANEXAMIC ACID → Blocks UV Signal → Melanin Production Interrupted"]*

**Pathway three — Niacinamide.**
Even if melanin gets produced, it still has to travel from the cells that make it to the skin cells on the surface. Niacinamide blocks that transfer. So even if pathways one and two miss some melanin, pathway three stops it from reaching your skin.

*[On-screen overlay: "3. NIACINAMIDE → Blocks Melanin Transfer → Pigment Stays Below Surface"]*

**Pathway four — Sodium Ascorbyl Phosphate.**
This is a stable form of Vitamin C. It neutralizes the free radicals that UV light creates — the same free radicals that trigger melanin production as a protective response. It also has its own mild tyrosinase inhibition. Think of it as backup for pathway one.

*[On-screen overlay: "4. VITAMIN C (SAP) → Neutralizes UV Free Radicals → Reduces Melanin Trigger"]*

**Pathway five — Glutathione.**
This one most creators would completely miss. Glutathione is a powerful antioxidant — and it actually shifts the type of melanin your skin produces. Your skin makes two types: dark eumelanin and lighter pheomelanin. Glutathione pushes that balance toward the lighter form. It does not just stop melanin — it changes what kind gets made.

*[On-screen overlay: "5. GLUTATHIONE → Shifts Dark Melanin → Lighter Melanin Produced"]*

## Filming Notes

- **Hold up five fingers when you say "five."** This is a visual anchor that creates the completion loop — viewers will stay to hear all five. Do not skip this gesture.
- **Each pathway needs its own beat.** Do not rush through all five. Give each one 10–15 seconds. The pace of the education section is what builds authority — rushing makes it feel like a list, not a pharmacist breakdown.
- **The Glutathione reveal is the strongest moment.** Pause slightly before "pathway five" — it is the most surprising and most pharmacist-exclusive point in the script. Let it land.
- **The mid-video rehook is critical.** At ~45 seconds, scroll-off risk is highest. The "but here is what most people don't know" line about glycolic acid re-engages viewers who are starting to drift.
- **The safety note is brief but important.** Do not skip it — it reinforces the pharmacist identity and protects against the "this made my skin worse" comment from someone who used it without SPF.
