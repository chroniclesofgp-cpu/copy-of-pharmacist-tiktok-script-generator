## Filming Notes

- **The credential is the hook — deliver it with confidence.** "I have been a pharmacist for over a decade" is the authority anchor. Do not soften it or rush past it. Let it land before moving to the reason.
- **The five-pathway education section should be delivered at a brisk but clear pace.** This is MOF → BOF — the viewer is already warm and does not need the same depth as the 5-Pathway Breakdown script (Script 22). Give each pathway one sentence, not a full explanation.
- **The objection handle for sensitive skin is important.** The essential oils and glycolic acid are real irritation risks. Addressing this proactively builds trust and reduces negative comments from people who had a reaction.
- **Best deployment:** Post this as the final video in the toner pad sequence — after Scripts 21, 22, 23, and 24 have built awareness and educated the audience. This is the conversion video. It works best on viewers who have already seen your other content and are in buying mode.
- **This is also a strong standalone video** for viewers who find you through search or the For You Page and are already in the "which dark spot product should I buy" decision stage.
