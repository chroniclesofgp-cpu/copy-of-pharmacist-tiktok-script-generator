## The Verdict (Pharmacist Conclusion)

**These two products target the same problem — dark underarms, bumps, and uneven tone — but through completely different mechanisms. There is a clear winner depending on your skin type and your primary concern.**

| Factor | Medicube | Truly Soft Serve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary brightening mechanism** | Kojic acid — tyrosinase inhibitor (blocks melanin production at the enzyme level) | Vitamin C — antioxidant brightener (reduces existing oxidative pigmentation) |
| **Exfoliation** | None (no AHA/BHA) | 5% AHA (lactic acid + mandelic acid) — active exfoliation |
| **Skin barrier support** | Strong — Ceramide NP, Shea Butter, Panthenol, Centella Asiatica | Minimal — Glycerin only |
| **Niacinamide** | Yes | Yes |
| **Odor control mechanism** | Magnesium Hydroxide (natural odor neutralizer) | AHA lowers skin pH — inhibits odor-causing bacteria |
| **Sensitive skin suitability** | High — low-irritation tested, no AHA, ceramide-fortified | Lower — 5% AHA can cause stinging on freshly shaved skin |
| **Fragrance** | Yes (vanilla & pistachio) | Yes (strawberry, vanilla, amber) |
| **Aluminum-free** | Yes | Yes |
| **Baking soda-free** | Yes | Not confirmed free of baking soda |
| **Price** | ~$24 (TikTok Shop) | ~$22 (TikTok Shop) |
| **Reviews (TikTok Shop)** | 1,161 reviews / 4.4 stars / 27.3K sold | 183 reviews / 4.5 stars / 1.8K sold |

**Clear winner for sensitive skin or post-shave use:** Medicube. No AHA means no stinging after shaving. The ceramide and shea butter actively repair the skin barrier while the kojic acid blocks new melanin production. This is the pharmacist pick for anyone whose skin is reactive or who shaves frequently.

**Clear winner for exfoliation + brightening in one step:** Truly Soft Serve. The 5% AHA exfoliates dead skin cells (which is where a lot of the visible darkening lives) while the vitamin C and niacinamide address tone. If you have thick, keratinized underarm skin and your primary complaint is texture and dullness — not irritation — this does more in one step.

**The pharmacist framing:** These are not interchangeable. Medicube is a skin-repair + brightening deodorant. Truly is a brightening + exfoliating deodorant. If you have sensitive skin, recently shaved, or have active bumps — Medicube. If your skin is not reactive and you want faster visible results on tone and texture — Truly.

**Both links should be provided.** This is a genuine situational split — not a close call where one wins on all dimensions.
