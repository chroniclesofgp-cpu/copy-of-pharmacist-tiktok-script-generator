## FULL SCRIPT

**[HOOK]**
> *"If you have tried kojic acid or turmeric and did not see results — you were not doing it wrong. You just did not know this."*

**[AUTHORITY]**
> *"I'm a pharmacist, and this is one of the most common reasons people give up on brightening ingredients that actually work."*

**[EDUCATION — mechanism]**
> *"Here is what kojic acid and turmeric actually do. They block the enzyme your skin uses to produce melanin — the pigment responsible for dark spots. When you apply them consistently, you are shutting down the production of new pigment. But here is the part nobody explains — they do not remove the pigment that is already there."*

**[VISUAL CUE: Graphic — "Kojic acid → blocks tyrosinase → stops NEW melanin production"]**

**[GAP — cell turnover]**
> *"The existing dark spots only fade as your skin naturally sheds and renews itself. That full cycle takes 28 to 40 days — and you need multiple cycles before you see a visible difference in tone."*

**[VISUAL CUE: Timeline — "Cycle 1 (week 4): production slowing. Cycle 2 (week 8): tone beginning to shift. Cycle 3 (week 12): visible brightening"]**

**[HOW TO USE]**
> *"Three things that matter. Apply it to completely dry skin — moisture dilutes the active and reduces absorption. Apply it as your last step before getting dressed so it has direct contact time with the skin. And use it every single day — skipping days allows the melanin production cycle to restart. You are resetting the clock every time you miss an application."*
