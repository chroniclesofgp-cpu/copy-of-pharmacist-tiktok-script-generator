# SCRIPT 52 — Medicube Bye-Bye Bumps Red Body Set
## One-Person Patient Dialogue: “I Got You” Bundle-Value Format

**Funnel stage:** MOF bundle-value / routine-clarity  
**Runtime target:** 48–55 seconds  
**Framework:** Product-specific adaptation of the [@justanothernurse1 “I got you” bundle format](https://www.tiktok.com/@justanothernurse1/video/7681419959360163085). This is a distinct rapid hand-off format, not a replacement for the slower Bundle Routine Walkthrough or the required BOF Type 3 asset.  
**Exact product:** [medicube Bye-Bye Bumps Red Body Set](https://shop.tiktok.com/us/pdp/1732553979595952363)  
**Primary buyer payoff:** A three-step body-care routine with distinct jobs, presented as one coordinated set rather than three items the viewer has to select separately.  
**Buyer-psychology sequence:** Recognition → pharmacist authority → mechanism clarity → routine confidence → convenience/value → conditional-cart action.  
**Reference materials:** `product-intel/medicube-bye-bye-bumps-red-body-set-fact-record.md`; `analysis/MEDICUBE_ONE_PERSON_PATIENT_DIALOGUE_FORMAT_PROPOSAL_2026-09-06.md`; `research/JUSTANOTHERNURSE_GOLI_I_GOT_YOU_VERBATIM_TRANSCRIPT_2026-09-06.md`.

> **Filming setup:** One creator plays both positions. The “patient” turns or leans in from screen left; the pharmacist stays screen right in a white coat and hands each real component toward the lens. Use literal “my patients” only if it truthfully reflects ordinary counseling conversations. Otherwise substitute **“when people tell me”** in the authority line.

## SCRIPT

### [0:00–0:03] LOOP 1 — ROUGH, BUMPY-LOOKING LEGS AFTER SHAVING

**PATIENT ROLE** *(screen left; quick, frustrated)*  
“I shave my legs, and they still look rough and bumpy.”

**PHARMACIST ROLE** *(screen right; immediately hand the Peeling Shot toward camera)*  
“As a pharmacist, when my patients tell me that, I got you.”

**PHARMACIST ROLE** *(hold Red Body Peeling Shot Cleanser)*  
“This is the rinse-off exfoliating step. Its 32% AHA, BHA, PHA, and LHA complex helps loosen the surface buildup that can leave body skin feeling rough.”

**PHARMACIST ROLE** *(show dry-skin application gesture; do not apply to face)*  
“Spray it on dry body skin, leave it for one minute, then rinse—and patch test first.”

### [0:12–0:21] LOOP 2 — BUMPY BACK OR CHEST

**PATIENT ROLE** *(screen left)*  
“Okay, but what about the bumps on my back and chest?”

**PHARMACIST ROLE** *(hand Red Body Wash toward camera)*  
“I got you. This body wash uses salicylic acid to help lift oil and dead-skin buildup while it cleans, with niacinamide to help support the skin barrier.”

**PHARMACIST ROLE** *(lather in hands or loofah)*  
“Lather, focus on the body areas you need it, then rinse.”

### [0:22–0:31] LOOP 3 — DRY, ROUGH FEEL AFTER THE SHOWER

**PATIENT ROLE** *(screen left)*  
“And what if my skin feels dry and rough again the second I get out of the shower?”

**PHARMACIST ROLE** *(hand Red Clear Capsule Body Lotion toward camera)*  
“I got you. This lotion has 5% urea, which helps pull moisture into dry skin and soften rough texture.”

**PHARMACIST ROLE** *(apply to arm/body area)*  
“Use this after you towel off so the routine ends with the leave-on moisture step.”

### [0:32–0:40] PATTERN BREAK — THE THREE-PRODUCT COST OBJECTION

**PATIENT ROLE** *(hold the three component products; skeptical)*  
“But how am I supposed to buy a peel, a wash, and a lotion separately?”

**PHARMACIST ROLE** *(hold the complete exact set together)*  
“Maybe individually. But Medicube put the peeling step, the wash, and the lotion together in one Bye-Bye Bumps set, so you are not trying to piece the routine together yourself.”

### [0:41–0:50] CLOSE — CURRENT BUNDLE / CART ACTION

**PHARMACIST ROLE** *(full set in frame; point down only on the final word)*  
“If you still see this exact set in the orange cart, I would take advantage while they are still offering it together.”
