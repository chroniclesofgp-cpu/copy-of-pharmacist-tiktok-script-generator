## FAITH ASSESSMENT — What She Missed and What to Keep

### What Faith Did Wrong (Do Not Replicate )

1. **She only linked one product.** She declared Loaded Tea the winner and put only that cart link up. This left Bloom buyers with no path to purchase — wasted conversion opportunity. Our version links both.

1. **She spent 40+ seconds on the sucralose/prebiotic contradiction.** This is a real pharmacist insight but it consumed nearly half the video on a single negative point about Bloom. By the time she got to Loaded Tea, the viewer was already in a skeptical mindset and the pivot felt like a sales pitch rather than a balanced comparison.

1. **No mechanism education for either product.** She never explained what L-theanine does, what galactomannan prebiotic fiber does, or why natural caffeine from green coffee bean is different from synthetic caffeine. She just listed features. The pharmacist credential was wasted — any influencer could have made that video.

1. **No on-screen ingredient popups or study overlays.** The entire video was talking-head with sticky notes. No visual reinforcement of the clinical claims.

1. **The "which one is right for you" framing was abandoned.** She set it up as a fair comparison but then picked a winner. The framing promised nuance and delivered a verdict. Viewers who prefer Bloom felt misled.

1. **Rambling in the Loaded Tea section.** She spent time on flavor count (40+ flavors), the launch price, and how to make it — none of which is pharmacist-level content. Every sentence must have a purpose.

### What Faith Did Right (Keep or Adapt)

1. **The sucralose/prebiotic contradiction is a genuine pharmacist insight.** It is clinically accurate and it is the kind of thing only a healthcare professional would catch. We keep this — but we compress it to 2 sentences and frame it as "the one thing to know" rather than spending 40 seconds on it.

1. **Showing the preparation on camera.** Pouring the Loaded Tea packet into a glass is a visual hook that makes the product feel real and accessible. We keep this as a filming note.

1. **The caffeine context (400mg daily limit, 200mg = half your day).** This is useful consumer education that builds trust. We keep it — compressed to one line.

1. **The "customizable" angle for Loaded Tea.** Bloom is a fixed 12oz can. Loaded Tea lets you control the dose and make 32oz. This is a real differentiator that Faith identified correctly. We keep it.
