**[AUTHORITY — 0:06–0:13]**
I'm a pharmacist. Dark circles are one of the most misunderstood cosmetic concerns because people treat them all the same way. The treatment for each type is different. Using the wrong product is why most people are frustrated with their results.

**[SYMPTOM CHECKLIST — 0:13–0:38]**
Type one: vascular dark circles. These are blue or purple — you are seeing blood vessels through the thin skin under your eye. They are worse when you are tired, when you have allergies, or when you have been crying. The treatment for vascular dark circles is caffeine — which constricts blood vessels — or vitamin K, which supports vascular integrity. Brightening ingredients do not help vascular dark circles. Type two: pigmentation dark circles. These are brown or yellowish-brown. They are caused by melanin deposition in the under-eye skin — often from sun exposure, genetics, or post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation. The treatment is a melanin inhibitor — niacinamide, kojic acid, or vitamin C. Type three: structural dark circles. These are shadows caused by volume loss and hollowing under the eye — the tear trough deepening as you age. The treatment is collagen restoration to restore volume, or filler in a clinical setting. Most dark circles are a combination of two or all three types.

**[HOOK — 0:00–0:07]**
This product claims to help with drooping eyelids. Most people either believe it completely or dismiss it completely. Here is the honest pharmacist explanation of when that claim is true — and when it is not.

**[AUTHORITY — 0:07–0:15]**
I'm a pharmacist. Ptosis — drooping eyelid — has multiple causes. And the type of ptosis determines whether a skincare product can help. This is a pharmacist-level triage that most creators skip.

**[MECHANISM — 0:15–0:48]**
There are two categories of drooping eyelid. The first is neurological or muscular ptosis — caused by nerve damage, a weakened levator muscle, Horner syndrome, or myasthenia gravis. This type requires medical evaluation. A skincare product cannot address neurological ptosis. If your eyelid drooping is sudden, affects one eye, or is accompanied by other symptoms, see a doctor. The second category is dermatological ptosis — drooping caused by loss of skin elasticity and collagen in the upper eyelid area. As the skin loses its structural support, it begins to fold and droop over the eyelid. This is the type that skincare can address — and this is what the brand is referring to. The REBORNIC calcium technology activates the Vitamin D receptor on fibroblasts in the upper eyelid skin, stimulating collagen and elastin synthesis. Improved collagen and elastin in the eyelid skin means improved structural support — which can reduce the appearance of dermatological drooping over time. This is a legitimate mechanism for the right type of ptosis. It is not a treatment for neurological ptosis.

The Dr. Melaxin Calcium Volume Eye Patch. The dual-calcium REBORNIC system for collagen and elastin synthesis in the eye area. 60 pads for $21. Use on the under-eye area and along the upper eyelid fold.

**[BRAND CLOSE + OBJECTION HANDLE — 1:00–1:10]**
Honest caveat: results for dermatological ptosis require consistent use over eight to twelve weeks. If your eyelid drooping is sudden or neurological in origin, this is not the right intervention — see a healthcare provider. Contains fragrance — patch test for sensitive skin.
