## Verified Facts (Do Not Change)

**Medicube:**

- Kojic acid — tyrosinase inhibitor. Blocks the enzyme that produces melanin. Prevents new pigmentation from forming.

- Ceramide NP, Shea Butter, Panthenol, Centella Asiatica — skin barrier repair

- Magnesium Hydroxide — natural odor control

- No AHA. Safe on freshly shaved skin.

- Aluminum-free, baking soda-free, paraben-free

**Truly Beauty Soft Serve:**

- 5% AHA — Lactic Acid + Mandelic Acid. Exfoliates dead skin cells on the surface (where visible darkening lives). Lowers skin pH to fight odor bacteria.

- Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid) + Niacinamide — brightens and evens tone

- Witch Hazel Extract — astringent, pore-tightening

- Aluminum-free

- Also targets ingrown hairs from shaving

**The protocol:**

- Truly: 2–3x per week (exfoliation days)

- Medicube: daily on the other days (prevention days)

- Do NOT use both on the same day — AHA-exfoliated skin is more permeable, layering actives the same day increases irritation risk

**The mechanism logic:**

- Truly removes existing darkened dead skin cells (surface-level, already there)

- Medicube blocks new melanin from forming (upstream, prevents new pigmentation)

- Together: clear what is already there + stop new pigmentation from forming = more complete protocol than either alone
