## What This Version Tests

v2 goes straight from symptoms → cortisol reveal → HPA axis explanation. This version inserts a **stress dysregulation bridge** between the cortisol reveal and the HPA axis education. The goal is to validate viewers who feel like their stress response is out of control — not just "stressed" — before explaining the mechanism. Riva's reframe ("not everybody's body handles stress the same way" ) is particularly strong for this audience.
