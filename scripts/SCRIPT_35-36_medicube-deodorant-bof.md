- Line 2 education: kojic acid works by blocking the tyrosinase enzyme — the enzyme that tells your skin to produce melanin. No tyrosinase activation means no new darkening.

- Line 3 education: ceramide NP repairs the barrier, kojic acid blocks new pigment, and the formula has no AHA — so it's safe to use immediately after shaving without irritation. That's barrier repair, brightening, and deodorizing in one product.

**Option 3 — Hyperpigmentation + Repeated Disappointment + Pharmacist Differentiator (Skeptical Viewer)**

*Short-form (SCRIPT_35):*

- Line 1: It's not because my patients who have tried every brightening product on the market and given up are finally seeing results they didn't think were possible without a prescription.

*Longer-form education lines (SCRIPT_36):*

- Line 2 education: the darkening is caused by barrier disruption from shaving triggering melanin overproduction. Ceramide NP repairs the barrier so the trigger stops. Kojic acid blocks the melanin enzyme. You're addressing both the cause and the pigment at the same time.

- Line 3 education: there is a published study showing kojic acid preparations significantly reduced hyperpigmentation — that's the kind of evidence I look for before I recommend something to my patients.

**Option 4 — Post-Shave Irritation + Razor Bumps + Daily Use Safety (Sensitive Skin Angle)**

*Longer-form education lines (SCRIPT_36):*
