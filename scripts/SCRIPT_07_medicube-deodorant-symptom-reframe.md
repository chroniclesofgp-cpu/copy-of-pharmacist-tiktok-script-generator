## FULL SCRIPT

**[HOOK]**
> *"If your underarms look like this, this, or this — it is not your genetics. It is not bad skin. You just have hyperpigmentation."*

**[AUTHORITY]**
> *"I'm a pharmacist, and dark underarms, bumps, and ingrown hairs are one of the most common skin concerns I get asked about — so if you're looking for answers about what's causing it and what you can do about it, you're in the right place."*

**[EDUCATION — the real cause]**
> *"Here is what is actually happening. Every time you shave, every time fabric rubs against your skin, every time a product irritates that area — your skin responds by producing extra melanin. That is called post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation. It is the same process that causes dark spots after a pimple."*

**[VISUAL CUE: Graphic — "Irritation → Melanin overproduction → Hyperpigmentation"]**

**[GAP — transition from education]**
> *"Your skin is not broken. It is doing exactly what it is designed to do. The problem is — the most common thing we put under our arms every single day is also one of the biggest triggers for this cycle. Most deodorants contain ingredients like baking soda, alcohol, and fragrance that irritate the skin barrier and keep that inflammation response going. And when you are shaving on top of that, the cycle never stops."*

**[EDUCATION CONT. — the brightening mechanism]**
> *"The ingredients that actually interrupt this cycle are tyrosinase inhibitors — compounds that block the enzyme responsible for producing melanin in the first place. Both kojic acid and turmeric have been shown to fade dark spots by blocking the enzyme that produces the pigment. Studies show both ingredients measurably reduce hyperpigmentation with consistent use."*

**[VISUAL CUE: Pop up study title card — kojic acid + tyrosinase inhibition (Lim JY et al. 2009)]**

**[SOLUTION BRIDGE — transition to product]**
> *"The issue has always been that there were no great options that could actually apply these brightening ingredients to the underarm area while also cutting out the irritants causing the problem in the first place. Medicube just changed that."*

**[PRODUCT INTRODUCTION]**
> *"Their Body Brightening Deodorant Stick has kojic acid and turmeric extract for the brightening, shea butter and niacinamide to repair the skin barrier — and it is completely aluminum-free and baking soda-free. So it is not adding new triggers while it is trying to fix the old ones. And it actually smells incredible — which honestly surprised me."*

**[VISUAL CUE: Product label close-up — ingredient list visible]**

**[MISDIRECTION]**
> *"Now — people ask how long it takes to see brightening. Honestly, you are looking at four to six weeks of consistent use. Melanin does not clear overnight. But the texture and bumps tend to improve faster than the tone — most people notice that first."*
