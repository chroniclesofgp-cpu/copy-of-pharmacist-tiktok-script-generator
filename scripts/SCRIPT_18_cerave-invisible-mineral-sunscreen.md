**[GAP — What Changed]**
CeraVe solved this with what they call Miner-ALL technology.

*(Pick up / hold up the CeraVe bottle — first product appearance)*

They surface-treat the zinc oxide particles with a silica coating — Triethoxycaprylylsilane — which changes how the particles interact with light. Instead of scattering it, they disperse it. The result is 15.35% zinc oxide that blends completely transparent on skin.

*(On-screen overlay: "Miner-ALL technology → surface-treated particles → no light scattering → transparent finish")*

They also added a photostabilizer called DESM — most sunscreens don't have this. As zinc oxide absorbs UV, it generates free radicals — and free radicals damage and irritate skin. DESM intercepts those free radicals before they reach your skin.

*(On-screen overlay: study popup — Sayre RM et al. 2006, Photochemistry and Photobiology, PMID 16492073 — "DESM quenches singlet oxygen, preventing UV filter photodegradation")*
