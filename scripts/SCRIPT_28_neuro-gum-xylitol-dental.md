## SCRIPT

**[HOOK — 0:00–0:06]**
The sweetener in this energy gum is doing something most people have no idea about — and it has nothing to do with energy.

**[AUTHORITY — 0:06–0:14]**
I'm a pharmacist. I counsel patients on supplements and medications every day. And when I look at an ingredient label, I'm not just looking at the actives — I'm looking at everything. And what I found in this one stopped me.

**[PROBLEM — 0:14–0:28]**
Most energy products are sweetened with sucralose or aspartame. Those are artificial sweeteners — and there is growing research suggesting they may disrupt the gut microbiome at higher doses. This gum uses neither. The sweetener is xylitol. And here is where it gets interesting.

**[REHOOK — 0:28–0:33]**
Because xylitol is not just a sweetener. It is an active ingredient — and your dentist knows exactly what it does.

**[MECHANISM — 0:33–1:00]**
If you have ever had a cavity, it was caused by a bacteria called Streptococcus mutans. That bacteria feeds on sugar, produces acid, and that acid is what dissolves your enamel. Xylitol cannot be fermented by Streptococcus mutans. The bacteria tries to metabolize it, cannot, and the process actually inhibits the bacteria's ability to stick to your teeth and form plaque. There is a published study in the Journal of the American Dental Association confirming that xylitol reduces the levels of Streptococcus mutans in saliva. So while you are chewing this gum for energy, the xylitol is actively working against the bacteria that cause cavities. That is a dental benefit built into an energy product — and the brand does not even market it.

**[GAP — 1:00–1:10]**
Every other energy gum or drink I have looked at uses sucralose. You are getting the caffeine, but you are also getting a sweetener with no dental benefit and emerging questions about gut health. This one made a different choice.

**[PRODUCT REVEAL — 1:10–1:25]**
The gum is Neuro Gum. Two pieces gives you 80 milligrams of caffeine — roughly one cup of coffee — plus 40 milligrams of L-theanine, which is the amino acid that promotes calm, focused alertness and prevents the jitter and crash. And the sweetener is xylitol. So this gum is doing three things at once: giving you energy, improving your focus, and protecting your teeth. I have not found another energy product that does all three.

**[BRAND CLOSE + OBJECTION HANDLE — 1:25–1:40]**
One thing to know — xylitol is extremely toxic to dogs. If you have a dog in the house, keep this away from them. That is not a small warning. Even a small amount of xylitol can cause a dangerous drop in blood sugar in dogs. Also — if you are caffeine-sensitive, start with one piece instead of two. One piece is 40 milligrams — about half a cup of coffee. You can adjust the dose by piece count, which you cannot do with a drink.

**[CTA — 1:40–1:50]**
I will link it below. If you see it in stock, I would grab it — this one moves fast. *(point down)*
