**[ROUND 1 — Speed of absorption]**

> *"Round one: how fast it works. When you drink coffee, the caffeine has to travel through your stomach and digestive tract before it reaches your bloodstream — that takes 30 to 45 minutes. Caffeine gum absorbs directly through the lining of your mouth. Studies show it can start working in as little as 5 to 10 minutes. And the difference in how that feels might surprise you."*

**[VISUAL CUE: On-screen text — "Coffee: 30–45 min | Gum: as fast as 5–10 min"]**

**[ROUND 2 — Jitters and crash]**

> *"Round two: the jitter and crash problem. Coffee gives you caffeine alone — most people know the feeling, the anxiety, the heart racing, the crash two hours later. Quality caffeine gum pairs the caffeine with L-theanine, an amino acid from green tea that promotes calm focus without sedating you. Coffee does not have this. And there is actually research on this specific combination."*

**[VISUAL CUE: Study pop-up — Giesbrecht et al. 2010, PubMed 21040626]**

**[ROUND 3 — Calories, staining, convenience]**

> *"Round three: everything else. A plain black coffee is fine. But most people are not drinking plain black coffee — they are drinking 300 to 400 calories of sugar and cream. Caffeine gum is zero calories, zero sugar, no aspartame, and it does not stain your teeth. It fits in your pocket."*
