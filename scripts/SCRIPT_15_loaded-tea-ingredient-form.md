## Filming Notes

- **Total estimated run time:** 60–70 seconds

- **Visual anchor:** The made drink is on screen from the first frame — colorful, appealing. The package is NOT shown until the CTA. This follows the TOF curiosity loop rule — product reveal is late.

- **Delivery tone:** Clinical and confident, not salesy. The hook is a pharmacist observation, not a pitch. The product reveal at the end should feel like a natural conclusion, not a transition into an ad.

- **Key delivery moment:** The line "That is not a marketing claim — that is pharmacology" is the authority peak of the video. Deliver it flat and matter-of-fact, not with emphasis. Confidence without performance.

- **On-screen text:** Each text overlay should appear as you say the corresponding line — not before. The study pop-ups should flash on screen for 2–3 seconds each.
