# VERSION A — Original Iteration (Mechanism-First )

*Strategy: Both reference creators list ingredients and let the viewer connect the dots. This version goes one level deeper — explains WHY each product's hero ingredient works at a cellular level, then uses that mechanism to make the winner feel like an obvious pharmacist conclusion rather than a sales pick. Reframes the comparison from "more ingredients" to "two fundamentally different cellular mechanisms — and once you understand that, the choice is clear."*

## Filming Notes (All Versions)

- **Split-face application** (Version B only): Apply Dr. Melaxin exclusively to your RIGHT side, Medicube exclusively to your LEFT side. This is the key visual technique from Faith's video.

- **Product comparison shot** (all versions): At some point hold both sticks fully twisted up side by side so the viewer can see Medicube has more product. Strong visual proof moment.

- **Text overlay pacing for Medicube ingredient list**: Popups should appear rapidly, one after another, as you name each ingredient. The visual stacking effect is what makes the list feel overwhelming in a good way.

- **Version A filming option**: Can be filmed with OR without the split-face application. The mechanism-first script works equally well with hold-and-show or split-face — your choice on the day.
