## SUPPORTING STUDIES (Verified — PMIDs confirmed June 23, 2026)

These studies support specific claims made across the 4 scripts. Use PubMed screenshots of these titles as visual overlays during mechanism sections.

**1. Vitamin D Receptor (VDR) and skin cell differentiation / turnover**

- **PMID 15156556** — Bikle DD et al. "Vitamin D regulated keratinocyte differentiation." *J Cell Biochem.* 2004. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15156556/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15156556/)

- Confirms VDR activation drives keratinocyte differentiation — the direct mechanism behind Rebornic's cell renewal effect

- Use during the Rebornic VDR mechanism explanation in Scripts 1 and 2

**2. Adenosine and collagen synthesis / anti-wrinkle**

- **PMID 18489289** — Sauermann K et al. "Evaluation of anti-wrinkle efficacy of adenosine-containing products using methods of modern biometrology." *J Cosmet Dermatol.* 2008. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18489289/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18489289/)

- Clinical study confirming adenosine reduces crow's feet and glabellar wrinkles — use for the adenosine brand close in Scripts 1 and 2

**3. Tremella Fuciformis (snow mushroom) and skin hydration**

- **PMID 32531816** — Lourith N et al. "Formulation and efficacy evaluation of the safe and efficient moisturizing snow mushroom hand sanitizer." *J Cosmet Dermatol.* 2021. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32531816/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32531816/)

- Confirms Tremella fuciformis polysaccharide significantly improves skin hydration vs. placebo — use for the hydration section in Script 2

**4. Topical collagen penetration limitation (molecular size)**

- **PMID 10839713** — Bos JD, Meinardi MM. "The 500 Dalton rule for the skin penetration of chemical compounds and drugs." *Exp Dermatol.* 2000. [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10839713/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10839713/)

- Establishes the 500 Dalton molecular weight limit for skin penetration. Collagen is ~300,000 Daltons — 600x too large. This is the foundational science behind the "collagen sits on the surface" claim in Scripts 2 and 4.
