## AUDIT SUMMARY (Complete)

| Status | Count | Notes |

### The Pattern Behind Every FAIL

Every ❌ FAIL hook follows one of two patterns:
1. **"Here is my pharmacist verdict on [product]"** — announces a verdict instead of delivering one. Appears in every expert-verdict script across all campaigns. This is a systemic writing error, not a one-off.
2. **Suppressed-knowledge hook that leads with the ingredient/product rather than the viewer's problem** — the "same compound as X" structure that positions the product as equivalent to a known alternative rather than superior to it.

### The Fix (Systemic)

The expert-verdict hook must always open with the verdict itself: **"This is the best [product] for [specific person or condition]."** This is the verbatim rphreviews pattern confirmed in 5/5 creators. The word "verdict" should never appear in the spoken hook — it belongs in the text overlay only, if used at all.
