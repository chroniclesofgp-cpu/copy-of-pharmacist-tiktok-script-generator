- Lead with: "The problem isn't that you're not brushing or flossing enough — it's that the bacteria in that plaque biofilm are producing toxins that inflame your gums. You need something that kills the bacteria, not just removes the visible buildup."

## KEY MECHANISM SECTIONS FROM EXISTING CAMPAIGN SCRIPTS

### From Video 2 (Thymol suppressed-knowledge) — use in both adaptations:
> "Thymol is a phenolic compound — it comes from thyme oil. Here is what it does: it inserts itself into the membrane of bacteria — the outer wall that holds the bacterial cell together. When thymol gets into that membrane, it increases the permeability — essentially punching holes in the wall. The bacteria cannot maintain their internal environment, and they die. This works against both gram-positive and gram-negative bacteria — which means it covers the full range of organisms responsible for plaque, gingivitis, and bad breath."

### From Video 1 (Hydrogen peroxide mechanism) — use in Script 2 adaptation:
> "Hydrogen peroxide releases free oxygen radicals. Those radicals break apart the double bonds in the chromogen molecules — and when those bonds break, the color disappears."

### From Video 2 (Product reveal with all four ingredients) — use in both:
> "It has thymol plus eucalyptus oil — which works synergistically with thymol, hitting the same bacterial membranes through a slightly different mechanism. Plus hydrogen peroxide for whitening and xylitol for cavity prevention. Three separate jobs happening in one rinse."
