# DryWater Campaign — Product-First Script Fidelity Map (Revised)

**Product:** [DryWater Raspberry Lemon, 12-stick TikTok Shop listing](https://shop.tiktok.com/us/pdp/1732265099914351437)  
**Status:** Active corrective map for the approved 10-asset campaign. It supersedes the first DryWater fidelity map following the documented root-cause audit.  
**Purpose:** Lock each script to a proven opening, a distinct buyer payoff, a non-repeating message hierarchy, source-aware proof, and a documented creator-style CTA before prose is drafted.

> **Core selling position:** For the person who is active or working in heat, dislikes syrupy sports drinks, and wants to know exactly what is in their packet, DryWater Raspberry Lemon offers a portable, fruit-forward electrolyte-and-vitamin mix with **0 g added sugar**, **less than 1 g total sugar**, four named fruit sources, an openly displayed multi-electrolyte panel, and an exact-product **NSF Certified for Sport** listing. The exact NSF listing identifies it as free of artificial colors, artificial flavors, and artificial sweeteners; recheck the current exact packet/PDP before filming.

This is a product-positioning statement, not a medical, rehydration-treatment, energy, performance, cramp-prevention, or universal-hydration promise.

## 2. Beat-Job Ledger — Non-Negotiable Pre-Draft Control

Each spoken beat receives **one** job and one new fact/payoff. It is cut or merged if it duplicates an earlier job without a different reason to buy.

| Beat job | What must be newly introduced | DryWater example of a valid job |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition / pain | A specific buyer moment or frustration. | “You work outside in the heat and every sports drink tastes like liquid candy.” |
| Category insight | One decision-relevant fact. | “A hydration packet is not just about the front label—the panel tells you what you are actually mixing.” |
| Product differentiator | One previously unused exact product fact tied to a buyer payoff. | “This one is 0 g added sugar and under 1 g total sugar, so the flavor does not have to come with a syrupy-drink tradeoff.” |
| Proof / quality signal | The label, a scoped study, a seller page, or a labeled shopper report. | “Here is the Raspberry Lemon panel: 380 mg sodium, 1,000 mg potassium, and the rest of the minerals listed out.” |
| Objection resolution | One distinct buyer barrier. | “Want something you can actually keep in the work bag? One stick goes into 12–16 oz.” |
| Retention | A new decision loop paid off by a new fact. | “But the fruit-forward taste is only half of why I picked this one.” |
| CTA | One direct cart action after the purchase case is established. | “If you want the Raspberry Lemon packet, it is in the orange cart while it is still there.” |

**Package rule:** A script may reference the pharmacist credential visually in every asset, but spoken authority must follow the selected framework. The literal phrase “I’m a pharmacist” is not generic filler and is never repeated in the body merely to restate authority.

## 5. Pre-Filming and Post-Write Gates

1. **Current exact product check:** Capture the Raspberry Lemon packet/PDP, ingredients, Nutrition Facts, seller, and cart immediately before filming.
2. **Product-message check:** Mark the primary buyer payoff and the unique differentiator for every spoken major beat. Delete a beat that does not add a new job or a new purchase reason.
3. **Repetition scan:** Water may appear in Script 1 and as the mixing medium in Script 7 only. Potassium/medication context may appear in Script 7 only. Spoken pharmacist authority follows the asset table, never default repetition.
4. **Proof check:** Use actual label/screenshots and clearly label reviews as individual buyer reports. Ingredient/category studies support only the limited mechanism or category statement stated in the fact record.
5. **Framework check:** Preserve the opening, reveal timing, section sequence, and CTA pattern documented for each selected framework. Do not replace them with generic “pharmacist verdict” language.
6. **Quality evidence:** The final Rule E audit must quote actual cuts/merges or record “none” for a specific reason. “Structural test passed” is separate from the writer’s substantive conversion review.
7. **Live-offer gate:** Scripts 8–9 are held unless the exact cart displays a real current deal/offer. No price, discount, sell-out, seller badge, review count, or sales count may be assumed from this document.
