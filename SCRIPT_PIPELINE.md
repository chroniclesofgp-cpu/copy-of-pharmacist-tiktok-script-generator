## How Priority Was Determined

Each opportunity was ranked on three factors:

1. **Proven hook** — Does a high-performing reference video exist (rphreviews, Riva, philsmypharmacist, etc.)? Proven hooks always outrank untested angles.
2. **Pharmacist-only angle** — Does the intel doc reveal an insight no non-credentialed creator can deliver? Higher uniqueness = higher priority.
3. **Product momentum** — Is this a product already in the content pipeline with affiliate links secured? Scripts for products you are actively promoting rank higher.
