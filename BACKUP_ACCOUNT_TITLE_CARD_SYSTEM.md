# Backup Account Recurring Title-Card System

## Purpose

A **recurring title-card style** is the small, recognizable visual system that appears at the beginning of every video. It is not a long branded introduction. It lets viewers recognize, within the first second, that the video belongs to the same trusted pharmacist account while the real hook remains the first spoken words.

The visual identity should feel like a **pharmacy-counter brief**: clean, direct, credible, and useful—not like a medical advertisement or a generic wellness template.

## Universal Build

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | 0.0–0.8 seconds for the series flash; the main text hook remains only through 3 seconds, then disappears. Never run a long intro. |
| **Base color** | Deep navy / near-black background or your real filming background, with a thin teal accent line. |
| **Series tag** | Small, all-caps label in the upper-left: `ASK A PHARMACIST`, `READ THE LABEL`, `TIER LIST`, etc. |
| **Main hook** | Large white text, 6–10 words maximum, centered or left-aligned in the upper half of the frame. This is the viewer-facing question—not the episode title. |
| **Credential badge** | Small lower-third: `LICENSED PHARMACIST • 10+ YEARS`. Use it from frame 0 or bring it in by 3 seconds. Do not show license number, employer name, or personal address. |
| **Motion** | Quick upward fade / slide, then clean exit. No spinning pills, stethoscope animations, or cluttered transitions. |
| **Typography** | One bold sans-serif for the hook; one clean condensed or mono font for the series tag and credential line. Use the same two fonts across every video. |

## Layout Formula

```text
UPPER-LEFT:  [ SERIES TAG ] ━━━━━

MAIN AREA:  THE VIEWER QUESTION / HIGH-STAKES HOOK

LOWER-LEFT: LICENSED PHARMACIST • 10+ YEARS
```

Use a real camera shot of you in the lab coat as the background whenever possible. The card should be an overlay on your real opening action—not a separate screen that delays the hook.

## Production Rules

1. **The title card never replaces the hook.** Your first spoken words still begin at 0:00.
2. **Use the series tag consistently, but change the main question every video.** Repetition builds recognition; changing the hook prevents viewers from tuning out.
3. **Keep the text hook short enough to read without pausing.** If it needs two sentences, it is a caption—not a title card.
4. **Remove the large hook text at 3 seconds.** Leave only a subtle series tag or credential badge if helpful.
5. **Do not put a product, price, orange cart, link language, or sales claim in the title card.** The backup account is building trust first.

## First Video Execution

For Launch Video 1, use the teal **ASK A PHARMACIST** tag. Start with the four generic containers in your hands, the main hook **“The 1 question before any OTC answer,”** and the lower-third **“LICENSED PHARMACIST • 10+ YEARS.”** At 3 seconds, remove the large text and go straight into the educational overlays.
