# Fact-Only Product Research Protocol

**Status:** Active clean-slate product-research standard  
**Effective:** September 1, 2026  
**Applies to:** Every new TikTok Shop product, product variant, bundle, or comparative product considered for an RxContent campaign.

> **Purpose:** Build a verifiable product record first. Research captures what the product is, what it contains, what its sources actually say, how it is used, what buyers ask, what comparable alternatives viewers know, and what evidence supports ingredient education. Campaign planning and script writing are separate later steps.

## 1. Clean Fact Boundary

This protocol preserves **facts, sources, and source scope**. It does not create a script, decide a verdict, invent a ranking, or translate a source into final filming language.

| Record type | Permitted content | Excluded content |
|---|---|---|
| **Product fact** | Exact product title, variant, seller, price observed at research time, package/label text, directions, warnings, full ingredients, and current listing/PDP claims | Inferences that a product produces a study outcome solely because it contains an ingredient |
| **Ingredient evidence** | Study population, ingredient/form/dose, mechanism, outcome, limitation, citation, and plain-language education opportunity | Claims that the studied outcome was demonstrated for the exact product when it was not tested |
| **Shopper intelligence** | Directly observed review/comment questions, complaints, motivations, stated use cases, and verbatim top comments | Invented “common” concerns, invented social proof, or conclusions drawn from too few observations |
| **Creator/video intelligence** | Exact URL, observed views/engagement, Visual + Spoken + Text hook, runtime, sequence, proof style, CTA, and verbatim top comments | A transcript, comment, or visual detail not actually observed |
| **Campaign decision** | Deferred until the fact record is complete and separately approved | Scripts, ratings, proposed language, or a product recommendation inside this fact record |

The sole standing script-level safeguard remains natural sentence-level qualification where appropriate, such as **“may help,” “helps support,” “can help,” “is used to help,”** or **“has been studied for.”** It is not applied during research by deleting evidence, mechanisms, buyer problems, or source facts.

## 2. Light Screen — Every New Link

Run this first for every new TikTok Shop link or brand product page. The output is a short **Light Fact Screen**. It decides only whether a Full Fact Record is needed.

| Required field | Capture standard |
|---|---|
| Exact identity | Brand, complete product title, SKU/size/variant, package form, TikTok Shop product link, seller/store identity, and research timestamp |
| Label/PDP proof | Current package or label source, full disclosed ingredient panel, official directions, warnings, and brand/listing claims exactly as presented |
| Buyer problem | The plain-language need demonstrated by the brand’s own framing and directly observed buyer/review language |
| Initial objections | Directly observed complaints, hesitations, safety questions, price friction, or routine barriers—include count/source where thin |
| Ingredient signal | Every brand-promoted, clinically studied, patented, or unusual ingredient/form worth a Full Fact Record investigation |
| Product decision | `Proceed to Full Fact Record` / `Need source verification` / `Do not promote` with a one-sentence fact-based reason |

Never substitute a typical category formula, an old variant, or a different retailer’s product for the exact current product. If the exact package label is unavailable, record the gap and do not write ingredient-specific campaign claims until it is resolved.

## 3. Full Fact Record — Required Before a Campaign Plan

Create `/product-intel/[product-name]-fact-record.md` only after the product passes the Light Screen. Every entry must retain a direct source URL, capture date, and scope note.

### 3.1 Product Identity and Source Ledger

Record the exact product/variant, current TikTok Shop link and seller, official PDP, physical package or current label source, authoritative retailer page if needed, and each access date. State which source controls when sources conflict: **the exact physical package or official current label controls formula, directions, and warnings.**

### 3.2 Verified Product Facts

Capture full ingredients in package order; disclosed concentrations; product form; exact directions; storage; warnings; population restrictions; official “free-from” statements; certifications; price/offer as observed; and any unresolved source conflict. Keep product claims separate from clinical evidence.

| Fact or claim | Exact source | Source type | Exact product or ingredient-level? | Scope / limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Fact] | [URL or package record] | Label / PDP / TikTok listing / retailer | Product / ingredient | [Boundary] |

### 3.3 Ingredient and Evidence Map

Research only ingredients actually verified on the exact product label or current official source. For every potential on-camera ingredient, record:

| Ingredient/form | Why it matters to the buyer problem | Evidence type | Best verified source | What the evidence supports | What it does **not** establish for this product | Potential proof-card takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Ingredient] | [Plain-language relevance] | Human study / systematic review / mechanism / label | [Citation] | [Scope] | [Boundary] | [Source-scoped viewer language] |

Use PubMed or other primary/authoritative sources for scientific evidence. Preserve the study’s population, dose/form, measured outcome, and limitations. A product’s inclusion of an ingredient does not alone prove the study outcome for that product.

### 3.4 Shopper Intelligence

Review TikTok Shop first, then official or major retailer reviews only where current and accessible. Separate direct observation from broad inference.

| Source | Sample observed | Purchase motivation | Complaint/limitation | Pre-purchase question | Confidence/limit |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| [TikTok Shop] | [Count] | [Observed language/theme] | [Observed language/theme] | [Observed language/theme] | [Sample/access limitation] |

### 3.5 Verbatim Top-Comment Bank

For the exact product’s highest-view accessible videos and current TikTok Shop listing, capture the **top 3–5 relevant, high-engagement comments** where visible. Preserve the comment exactly as written, the source video/listing, observed engagement context, and the buyer question or objection it reveals. Do not turn a comment into a product claim or present it as if it originated under the creator’s own video.

| Rank | Exact source video/listing | Original commenter | Verbatim comment | Observed comment engagement/context | Buyer insight | Approved authentic downstream use |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [URL] | [Visible handle or “not preserved”] | “…” | [Likes/replies/pinned status if visible] | [Motivation / objection / question] | Comment-reply topic / viewer-language hook / creator-pinned clarification / FAQ or comparison input |

The bank exists to preserve the market’s own language and make later objection handling, comment-reply videos, authentic pinned clarifications, and follow-up content easier. It does **not** authorize fabricated comments, manufactured testimonials, or represented-as-organic audience engagement.

### 3.6 Top-5 Exact-Product Video Benchmark

Before a campaign plan, inspect the **3–5 highest-view accessible videos for the exact product** at research time. Use public observed views as a reach signal, not proof of sales. If fewer than three exact-product videos exist, document the gap and separately capture category videos—never relabel them as product winners.

For every benchmark video, watch the accessible video through its full sequence where possible and record:

| Rank by observed views | Creator and URL | Observed views/engagement | Visual + Spoken + Text hook | Runtime and product-reveal timing | Format / sequence / proof behavior | CTA / cart behavior | Real comments or unanswered question | Source limit |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Link] | [Observed metric] | [Observed] | [Observed] | [Observed] | [Observed] | [Observed] | [Limit] |

Then conduct a separate **credentialed healthcare creator audit** where relevant. Product-specific high-view videos identify what holds attention; credentialed examples identify how pharmacy/clinical authority can add value. Neither is copied without a current product fact match.

### 3.7 Competitive Decision Map

Complete the active [`Competitive Decision Map`](analysis/rebuild-frameworks/COMPETITIVE_DECISION_MAP_STANDARD_2026-09-01.md) before any comparison, Category Scorecard, Numeric Scorecard, Best-Last List, or Tier List is considered. Research real buyer alternatives—products, forms, procedures, therapies, professional options, or common routines—with their fair role and source-supported limitation/frustration.

| Alternative | Type | Why buyers know/use it | Narrow helpful role | Source-supported limitation/frustration | Viewer gap left open | TikTok Shop link status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Alternative] | Product / procedure / therapy / routine | [Source] | [Fair role] | [Source] | [Gap] | Linked / unlinked / not applicable |

Two or more 8/10+ products may later be linked only if each is currently available and serves a distinct viewer need. The fact record establishes the decision set; it does not assign ratings.

### 3.8 Pharmacist Value and Use Instructions

Record the pharmacist-relevant source facts: a mechanism that needs translation, precise application/use reason, ingredient-form distinction, population warning, interaction, or product-versus-procedure distinction. This is a fact opportunity—not a prewritten script.

For instructions, capture exact current directions, frequency, route/area, precautions, and the source controlling each. Preserve practical pharmacist context separately from instructions. Do not manufacture a warning where no source supports one.

### 3.9 Fact-Record Completion Gate

A Full Fact Record is complete only when all of the following are true:

1. The exact product identity, seller/store, current link, and controlling package/PDP source are recorded.
2. Ingredients, directions, and warnings are verified for the exact current variant or explicitly marked unavailable.
3. Every potential ingredient mechanism has a source and stated evidence scope.
4. Shopper intelligence records observed evidence and sample limitations.
5. The Verbatim Top-Comment Bank captures the visible top 3–5 relevant comments or explicitly documents why adequate comments were unavailable.
6. The Top-5 Exact-Product Video Benchmark is complete or the shortage is explicitly documented.
7. The Competitive Decision Map is complete for decision-format eligibility.
8. No script, score, campaign verdict, or recycled post-trial strategy language appears in the fact record.

## 4. Campaign Planning Gate — Separate Approval

Only after the Fact-Record Completion Gate may a campaign plan be proposed. The plan must cite the current fact record and selected fresh framework card, identify its Product X-tested structure, record the real buyer problem, named buyer-psychology levers, proof-card source, and filming-day cart condition. It requires user approval before scripting. The shared decision-layer authority for asset selection, BOF planning, sequencing rationale, and cross-script discipline is `CAMPAIGN_PLANNING_PROTOCOL.md`; this fact-only protocol remains the source authority for the research record itself.

## 5. Active Source Set by Task

| Immediate task | Required source set |
|---|---|
| Light Fact Screen | `MASTER_CONTEXT.md`, this protocol, exact current TikTok Shop/PDP/package source, and direct review access where available |
| Full Fact Record | Light Fact Screen sources, physical label/official product sources, relevant primary evidence, Top-5 product videos, comments, and Competitive Decision Map |
| Campaign plan | `MASTER_CONTEXT.md`, `CAMPAIGN_PLANNING_PROTOCOL.md`, completed fact record, current rebuilt framework card, buyer-psychology reference, and original creator source/reference video |
| Script writing | All six full script-writing sources, the selected framework, completed fact record, approved campaign plan, and original source/reference materials |

After any context compression, reload the full source set named for the immediate task before continuing.

## 6. Product Updates and New Variants

Keep the fact record, not its past interpretation. When a product changes seller, package, formula, price, offer, bundle contents, or TikTok Shop listing, append a dated source update and re-evaluate only the affected fact, evidence, decision-map, campaign, or script dependency. Do not assume an older record controls a new variant.
