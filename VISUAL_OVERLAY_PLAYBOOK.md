## HOW TO USE THIS PLAYBOOK

When writing a script, read **Part 5 (Script-Level Assignment)** to determine which overlay strategy to assign. Every script ends with a POST-PRODUCTION NOTES section specifying the assigned strategy. This playbook is the reference — the script is where the assignment lives.

---

## PART 1 — THE MOST IMPORTANT FINDING

**No creator in the 84-video dataset uses a text overlay that stays on screen for the entire video.** There is no "persistent banner" in any of the analyzed videos. Previous versions of this document incorrectly described the rphreviews opening hook text as a persistent element. It is not. It disappears after 3–8 seconds.

What creators use instead is a **layered, timed overlay system** where different elements appear and disappear at specific moments. Each overlay serves a specific function at a specific point in the video. The cumulative effect is a video that feels visually dense and credible, but no single text element stays on screen the whole time.

**The only truly persistent visual element is the physical environment and costume.** White coat, credential badge clipped to coat, bookshelf with health books, products on desk, diplomas on wall — these are always-on authority signals that require no overlay work. Naturo's bookshelf background and your diploma wall both function this way. A creator with a strong physical credential environment needs fewer text overlays than a creator filming in a plain setting.

## PART 2 — UNIVERSAL OVERLAY RULES (Confirmed 5/5 Creators)

These patterns appear in all five creators across all 84 videos. They are structural requirements.

| Rule | What It Is | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Credential visual from frame 0** | Some form of authority signal visible before or alongside the first spoken word | 5/5 creators, 100% of videos |
| **PubMed/study screenshots** | Journal article titles flashed on screen during mechanism section | 5/5 creators, varies per creator |
| **Physical product hold-up at reveal** | Product physically brought into frame, never a static image | 5/5 creators, 100% of videos |
| **Physical downward-pointing gesture at CTA** | Creator physically points finger down toward cart button | 5/5 creators, 90%+ of videos |
| **Stock-based scarcity in CTA** | "Before it runs out," "if you still see it in stock," "snag it while you can" | 5/5 creators, 100% of videos |
| **Supply duration framing — never price** | Value communicated through "4-month supply," "300 capsules," never a dollar amount | 5/5 creators, 100% of videos |
| **Quality proof anchor** | "No fillers / third-party tested / made in USA" — verbal or visual | 5/5 creators, 90%+ of videos |
| **Education before product reveal** | Mechanism/education section always precedes the product recommendation | 5/5 creators, 100% of videos |
| **Proactive objection handling** | 2–4 objections addressed before the viewer forms them | 5/5 creators, 100% of videos |

## PART 4 — CREATOR-BY-CREATOR OVERLAY SYSTEMS

### rphreviews — The Standardized System
**Dataset:** 23 videos analyzed | **Total GMV:** $694K | **Best video:** $97K (Nasomin)

rphreviews has the most systematized overlay approach in the dataset. His system is formula-based — the same elements appear in the same positions across 20+ videos.

**Opening hook text (17/23 videos):**
Red pill-shaped or rectangular text box, full-width or near-full-width across the top of the screen. Formula: *"[If you have X / If you want X] — You MUST watch this video ⚠️"* Duration: **3–8 seconds**, then disappears completely. The red color is a psychological alarm signal that primes the viewer for urgency before the spoken hook begins.

Confirmed examples from his top videos:
- "If you want to take collagen You MUST watch this video ⚠️" (NMN/Collagen — $59K)
- "You need magnesium for your health You MUST watch this video ⚠️" (Magnesium — $72K)
- "#1 pharmacist 👨🏻‍⚕️ recommendations to prevent 🛑 you from getting sick 🤒" (Nasamine — $97K)
- "If you have undereye hallows You MUST watch this video ✅" (The Ordinary vs. Medicube — $81K)

**Throwback pharmacy photo (19/23 videos):**
A photo of him younger, in a pharmacist coat with name tag, in a retail pharmacy. Appears for 1–2 seconds during the verbal credential ("I've been a pharmacist for over 17 years"). Proves the credential predates TikTok — implies no financial motivation to fabricate it.

**Condition/symptom images (4/5 top videos):**
Close-up photos of the skin condition or symptom being addressed, appearing during the hook or problem section. Each image shown for 2 seconds. Medicube Balm video opens with 4 sequential condition images (nasolabial folds, under-eye hollows, forehead wrinkles, sagging neck) in the first 10 seconds. The viewer sees their own face in these images.

**Sequential checkmark benefit stack (18/23 videos):**
After the product reveal, benefit overlays with ✅ symbols stack vertically. Each benefit appears for 1–2 seconds and stays on screen as the next one appears below it. Covers 4–8 benefits over 8–15 seconds total. Creates cumulative value signal — each checkmark adds to perceived product value.

**Study screenshots (11/23 videos):**
Placed in the mechanism section only. Shown for 2–3 seconds each. 1–2 studies per video on average.

**Quality certification cluster (14/23 videos):**
Three badges shown simultaneously in the pre-CTA section: GMP Certified + Third Party Tested + Made in USA. Visual cluster, no verbal explanation needed.

**TikTok Shop screenshot (9/23 videos):**
Product listing with star rating and units sold. Placed in pre-CTA section. Shown visually AND stated verbally simultaneously.

**CTA arrow (20/23 videos):**
Large colored arrow (pink, yellow, orange, or red) pointing downward. Animated (bouncing/pulsing) in 8/23 videos. Appears simultaneously with verbal CTA, combined with physical finger-pointing gesture.

### Drew — The Scientific Authority System
**Dataset:** 20 videos analyzed across 2 accounts | **Total GMV:** $1.15M | **Best video:** $242K (collagen)

Drew's overlay system is the most academically dense in the dataset. Her primary technique is the PubMed wall — volume of scientific evidence that creates overwhelming proof without requiring the viewer to read any of it.

**Lab coat + ID badge (20/20 videos):**
Visible from frame 0 throughout the entire video. This is not a text overlay — it is a costume element. But it functions as the most powerful always-on credential signal in the dataset. No text overlay needed because the visual credential is always present.

**PubMed wall (20/20 videos):**
8–15 study screenshots per video during the mechanism section. Rapid-fire pace — each study shown for 1–2 seconds. The viewer cannot process each study individually, but the cumulative visual impression is overwhelming scientific validation. This is Drew's signature technique.

**Zero-overlay video (Drew Acct1 Astaxanthin — $179K, her highest-GMV video):**
This video used no text overlays at all. Just verbal delivery, lab setting, and physical product hold-up. This is the most important data point in the dataset: the right verbal hook in the right physical setting can outperform a heavily overlaid video. Drew's physical environment (lab coat, ID badge, clinical setting) did all the credential work.

**Ingredient label proof (12/20 videos):**
Shows the actual supplement facts panel on screen after the product reveal. Proves the product contains the ingredients she discussed.

### Riva — The Warm Authority System
**Dataset:** 22 videos analyzed | **Total GMV:** ~$850K | **Best video:** $218K (magnesium)

Riva's overlay system is the most balanced in the dataset — widest variety of overlay types and highest frequency of review popups.

**Opening text hook banner (18/22 videos):**
White text on dark semi-transparent overlay banner. Formula: *"[INGREDIENT/CONDITION IN CAPS] — [Consequence/Action in Title Case] ⚠️"* Duration: 3–8 seconds, then disappears.

**Pharmacy license popup (15/22 videos):**
Screenshot or graphic of her actual pharmacy license. Appears for 2–3 seconds during verbal credential delivery (0:08–0:15). Proves credential is real and pre-existing.

**Review popup sequence (19/22 videos):**
3–5 five-star reviews appearing in rapid succession, 1–2 seconds each. Each review highlights a different benefit. Creates impression of overwhelming consensus while keeping the viewer engaged through rapid visual change. This is Riva's most distinctive overlay technique.

**Nutrient comparison charts (8/22 videos):**
"23x more iron than spinach" style graphics. Makes abstract supplement benefits concrete. Placed in mechanism section.

**TikTok Shop overlay with units sold (17/22 videos):**
Placed at or near CTA. Shown visually AND stated verbally.

### Naturo — The Environmental Authority System
**Dataset:** 9 videos analyzed | **Total GMV:** $600K+ | **Best video:** $216K (astaxanthin)

Naturo's overlay system relies heavily on environmental authority. Her bookshelf background does more credibility work than any text overlay. Her text overlays are front-loaded and disappear early. Retention is driven by content value and open loops, not visual pattern interrupts.

**"From A Certified Naturopath" credential badge (9/9 videos):**
Present from frame 0. White or yellow text on dark background. Upper third of screen. Disappears after 3–5 seconds. Then the environmental credential (bookshelf, diplomas) takes over.

**Environmental authority (9/9 videos):**
Bookshelves with health/science books, supplement bottles, framed certificates/diplomas visible in background throughout the entire video. Always-on passive credential — functions without any overlay.

**PubMed study screenshots (9/9 videos):**
6–10 per video. Rapid-fire flash, 1–2 seconds each. "Overwhelming Evidence" technique — viewer cannot read them but sees the volume.

**Antioxidant comparison bar chart (5/7 astaxanthin videos):**
"6000x stronger than Vitamin C" style graphic. Placed in mechanism section. Reusable across astaxanthin videos.

**"Synergistic Dual-Action" custom graphic (4/5 bundle videos):**
Custom graphic showing NMN as "fuel" and Astaxanthin as "protection." Appears at the "you need both" reveal moment. Makes the bundle argument visually compelling. Built once, reused across multiple videos.

**Dual CTA visual (7/9 videos):**
Red graphic arrow pointing down + physical finger-pointing gesture simultaneously. Most aggressive CTA visual design in the dataset.

**Celebrity reference images (4/9 videos):**
Kim Kardashian, Christina Aguilera, Justin Bieber, Joe Rogan. Placed in first 30 seconds as trend validators — not product endorsements.

## PART 5 — SCRIPT-LEVEL OVERLAY ASSIGNMENT

When writing a script, assign one of the following strategies. Include the assignment in the POST-PRODUCTION NOTES section at the end of the script.

### Strategy A — rphreviews Standard Package
**Best for:** Single-ingredient education videos, deficiency-based products, fear/urgency angle, plain filming background

**Elements:**
1. Opening hook text (red banner, 3–8 seconds): Write the exact text in the script POST-PRODUCTION NOTES
2. Condition/symptom images (2–4 images, hook/problem section, 2 seconds each) — if applicable
3. Study screenshot (1–2 studies, mechanism section, 2–3 seconds each)
4. Checkmark benefit stack (4–6 benefits, post-reveal, 1–2 seconds each)
5. Quality certification cluster (pre-CTA, 3–5 seconds)
6. CTA arrow (final 10 seconds)
7. Physical downward point (always)

**Opening hook text formula:** *"If you [have X / want X] — You MUST watch this video ⚠️"*

---

### Strategy B — Drew Scientific Authority
**Best for:** Mechanism-heavy videos, ingredient education, science-forward angle, strong physical setup

**Elements:**
1. Credential badge or lab coat visible from frame 0 (physical, not overlay)
2. 4–8 study screenshots (mechanism section, 2–3 seconds each — use real PubMed screenshots only)
3. Ingredient label close-up (post-reveal, 3–5 seconds)
4. Quality proof verbal anchor ("no fillers, third-party tested, made in USA")
5. Physical downward point at CTA

**Note:** This strategy works best when you have actual PubMed screenshots to show. Do not use placeholder graphics. Drew's highest-GMV video ($179K) used zero overlays — if your physical setup is strong, this strategy can be run with minimal overlay work.

---

### Strategy C — Riva Warm Authority
**Best for:** Multi-symptom videos, condition-based products, relatable/personal angle, review-rich products

**Elements:**
1. Opening text hook banner (dark overlay, white text, 3–8 seconds): Write the exact text in POST-PRODUCTION NOTES
2. 1–3 study screenshots (mechanism section)
3. Review popup sequence (3–5 reviews, post-reveal, 1–2 seconds each)
4. TikTok Shop screenshot (units sold + stars, pre-CTA)
5. Physical downward point at CTA

**Opening hook text formula:** *"[INGREDIENT/CONDITION IN CAPS] — [Consequence/Action] ⚠️"*

---

### Strategy D — Naturo Environmental Authority
**Best for:** Bundle videos, comparison videos, astaxanthin/NMN/anti-aging products, strong physical background

**Elements:**
1. Credential badge text (3–5 seconds, then disappears)
2. 4–8 study screenshots (mechanism section, rapid-fire)
3. Comparison bar chart if applicable (e.g., "6000x stronger than Vitamin C")
4. "Synergistic Dual-Action" graphic if bundle video (at "you need both" moment)
5. Dual CTA visual: red arrow + physical finger point

**Note:** Requires a strong physical background (bookshelves, diplomas, products). If filming environment is plain, use Strategy A instead.

---

### Strategy E — Minimal (Strong Physical Setup)
**Best for:** Videos where your physical environment (diplomas, lab coat, products on desk) is doing the credential work; short videos under 60 seconds; comment reply videos; A/B test against heavier overlay strategies

**Elements:**
1. 1–2 study screenshots (mechanism section, 2–3 seconds each)
2. Product name label (text overlay at product reveal, 3–5 seconds)
3. Physical downward point at CTA
4. No opening hook text — physical environment handles credential

**When to use:** Your diploma wall + white coat + products on desk is the equivalent of rphreviews' throwback photo + credential badge. You do not need to add text overlays on top of a strong physical setup. This is the Drew approach — the costume and environment are the credential. Drew's $179K video proved this can outperform a heavily overlaid video.

## PART 6 — OVERLAY ASSIGNMENT DECISION TREE

Work through these questions in order when writing a script:

**Step 1: What is the video's primary angle?**
- Fear/urgency (deficiency, getting sick, missing out) → Strategy A
- Science/mechanism (here's how this works at a cellular level) → Strategy B
- Symptom/condition (if you have X, Y, Z symptoms) → Strategy C
- Bundle/comparison (A vs. B, you need both) → Strategy D
- Short video, strong physical setup, comment reply → Strategy E

**Step 2: How strong is the physical filming environment?**
- Strong (diplomas, bookshelves, lab coat, products on desk) → Can reduce overlay count; lean toward Strategy D or E
- Moderate (plain background, lab coat only) → Standard overlay count; Strategy A, B, or C
- Minimal (plain background, no coat) → Maximum overlay count; Strategy A or B

**Step 3: What assets are available?**
- PubMed screenshots available → Include study overlays (Strategies A, B, C, D)
- No PubMed screenshots → Verbal citation only; do not fake the visual
- Product listing with reviews available → Include TikTok Shop screenshot (Strategy C)
- Multiple products → Consider checkmark stack or "Synergistic Dual-Action" graphic

**Step 4: Is this an A/B test video?**
- If yes → Assign different strategies to A and B versions; note both in the script header
- If no → Assign one strategy; note it in POST-PRODUCTION NOTES

## PART 8 — OVERLAY TESTING FRAMEWORK

### Current Data State
The 84-video dataset gives us verified data on what works for five established creators. We do not yet have conversion data from your own account to compare against. Until your own data accumulates, the default strategy is to replicate the highest-frequency patterns from the dataset (Strategy A) while occasionally testing alternatives.

### Test Rotation Logic
Apply this rotation to maintain a primary strategy while generating test data:

| Video Count | Strategy | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Videos 1–3 of a product | Strategy A (rphreviews Standard) | Establish baseline |
| Video 4 | Strategy C (Riva Warm Authority) | Test review popup sequence |
| Video 5 | Strategy E (Minimal) | Test whether overlays add or subtract from your physical setup |
| Video 6+ | Return to whichever performed best | Exploit the winner |

### What to Log in SCRIPT_FEEDBACK_LOG.md
For every video, record:
- Strategy assigned
- Opening hook text (if used)
- Number of study screenshots included
- Whether checkmark stack was used
- GMV at 7 days and 30 days

### A/B Test Pairs
When writing two versions of the same script:
- Assign different strategies to A and B
- Keep all other variables identical (same hook, same script structure, same product)
- The overlay strategy is the only variable being tested
