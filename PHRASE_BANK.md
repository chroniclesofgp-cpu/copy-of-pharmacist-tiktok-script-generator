## How to Use This File

Each phrase is tagged with:
- **Creator** — who uses it
- **Validation** — `[UNIVERSAL]` = confirmed in 3+ creators, `[CONFIRMED]` = confirmed in 2 creators, `[SINGLE]` = one creator only
- **Frequency** — how often it appears across that creator's videos

When writing a script, pull 1–2 lines from each category as structural anchors. Do not copy verbatim — adapt the sentence structure to your product.

## 7. USAGE NOTES FOR SCRIPT WRITING

**Gap section:** Start with a "most people don't know" or "here's the thing" opener. Follow with the specific mistake or missing knowledge. End with a pivot line that makes the product feel like the logical next step.

**Transition:** Use "The one I always recommend" or "And that's why I recommend" — not "Buy this product." The transition should feel like advice, not a pitch.

**Objection handle:** Always include at least one quality proof line (USA + third-party tested). For pharmacist/doctor accounts, add the medical disclaimer near the CTA — it increases trust, not decreases it.

**Rehook:** If your video is over 45 seconds, add a "But here's the thing" or "But here's what most people don't know" at the 30-second mark. This is the highest scroll-off risk window.

**Caption — 5-creator synthesis:** The dominant pattern (4 of 5 creators) is: **[Full sentence summarizing the video's core claim or hook] + [4–6 hashtags]**. The sentence is not a CTA — it is a compressed version of the video's hook, designed to capture search and give context to viewers who see the caption before watching. rphreviews is the outlier (sentence + disclaimer, minimal hashtags) but is also the highest-revenue creator in the dataset. For pharmacist accounts, the rphreviews formula (symptom → @brand → disclaimer) is the highest-trust format. For non-pharmacist health/wellness creators, the Drew/naturo formula (hook sentence + 5–6 topical hashtags) is the cross-creator standard.

**Hashtag strategy — what the data actually shows:**
- Drew: avg 6.0 hashtags, 100% topical (#holistichealth, #womenshealth, #over40)
- naturo: avg 5.0 hashtags, always includes #resultsmayvary
- Dr. Faith: avg 4.6 hashtags, brand-heavy (#microingredients, #medicube) + TikTok Shop campaign tags
- rphreviews: avg 0.3 hashtags — relies entirely on natural-language SEO in the caption sentence
- Riva: avg 1.4 hashtags

**Practical rule for your account:** Use the rphreviews formula as the base (symptom sentence + @brand + disclaimer). Add 3–5 hashtags only if the product has a high-search ingredient (#magnesium, #nmn, #astaxanthin). Do not use more than 5 hashtags — the data shows diminishing returns above 5, and rphreviews' 0.3 average with the highest GMV suggests the sentence text is doing more SEO work than the hashtags.

## 8. CTA MECHANICS — TIKTOK SHOP SPECIFIC

### 8a. "If You See That Orange Cart" — TikTok Shop In-Stock Signal

**Validation: SINGLE (this creator's own filmed content — Video 7)**

This mechanic tells the viewer exactly what the orange TikTok Shop cart icon means and what to do when they see it. It is a TikTok Shop-specific conversion mechanic that transforms the cart icon from a passive UI element into an active scarcity/availability signal.

**Filmed version (Video 7, HiSmile iD Stain):**
> *"This works so well it racked up thousands of 5-star reviews and sold out multiple times. You can't even get them in stores right now. I'm going to link it down so if you see that orange cart, that means they did finally restock — so if you want to give it a try, it's a good time to grab one before they sell out again."*

**Structural breakdown:**

| Element | Line | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Social proof anchor | "racked up thousands of 5-star reviews" | Validates product quality before scarcity claim |
| Scarcity signal | "sold out multiple times" | Establishes demand history |
| Availability signal | "can't even get them in stores" | Exclusivity — TikTok Shop is the only source |
| Orange cart instruction | "if you see that orange cart, that means they did finally restock" | Teaches viewer what the icon means — removes friction |
| Soft CTA | "if you want to give it a try, it's a good time to grab one" | Low-pressure framing — viewer-driven, not pushy |
| Urgency close | "before they sell out again" | Closes the loop on the scarcity signal |

**Template for reuse:**
> *"[Product] has [X] 5-star reviews and has sold out [number] times. I'm going to link it below — if you see that orange cart, that means it's back in stock. If you want to try it, now's a good time to grab one before it sells out again."*

**When to use:**
- Products with documented stock history (sold out before, limited runs)
- Products exclusive to TikTok Shop (not available in stores)
