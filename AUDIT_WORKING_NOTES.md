## KEY PROVEN HOOK OPENING LINES (verbatim from database)

### expert-verdict (Hook 26) — 5/5 creators validated
- "This is the best [product] for [condition/person]."
- "This is the best kids multivitamin." — rphreviews Rank 19 ($20K+)
- "This is the best thing for your eyes." — rphreviews Rank 23 ($20K+)
- "This is the best thing for your prostate." — rphreviews Rank 22 ($20K+)
- Text hook: "The best [product] You MUST watch this video ⚠️" (Red banner)
- RULE: Verdict is the FIRST words. Credential comes AFTER. Never "Here is my pharmacist verdict on X" — that is a label, not a verdict.

### right-way (Hook 23) — 5/5 creators validated
- "Here's how to take [ingredient] the right way." — rphreviews Rank 17 ($21K+)
- "If you're taking collagen and not seeing results — you might be taking it wrong." — Riva Rank 14 ($31K)
- "So NMN was shoved down your throat by all the celebrities..." — Dr. Faith Rank 1 ($279K)
- Text hook: "How to take magnesium the right way You MUST watch this video ⚠️"

### suppressed-knowledge (Hook 3) — 3/5 creators validated
- "Nobody talks about this."
- "Listen carefully."
- "They don't want you to know..."
- "I don't gatekeep."
- RULE: Information itself is the authority signal. No verbal credential opener.

### symptom-checklist (Hook 2) — 4/5 creators validated
- "If you're losing hair, gaining weight, feeling exhausted, or struggling to sleep — your cortisol might be out of control." — Riva
- RULE: Visual badge only, NOT verbal authority opener. Go straight into symptoms.

### instruction-correction (Hook 1) — confirmed
- "Here's how to take [product/ingredient] the right way."
- Audience-callout variant: "So you are taking [PRODUCT], but you are not seeing [BENEFIT 1] or [BENEFIT 2]. Let me tell you what is going on — and here is how to take it the right way."

### fear-external-threat (Hook 25) — rphreviews only ($170K+ combined)
- Opens with NEWS CLIP (ABC News, CDC headline) — creator does NOT appear until 0:05
- Creator's first words: "Because unfortunately I've been a pharmacist for over 17 years and I know what I'm talking about."
- RULE: Requires genuine timely external threat. Cannot be manufactured.

### age-reversal (Hook 7)
- "If you're over [age], you need to know about this."
- "At [age], your body starts [doing X]."

### storytime (Hook 13)
- "I had a patient come in..." / "A woman came into my pharmacy..."
- RULE: Story must be first-person witnessed. No third-person distance.

### before-pill-bottle (Hook 14)
- "Before I reach for the pill bottle, I always try [X] first."
- RULE: Must include "long term" qualifier. Never say "stop taking your medication."

### dosing-authority (Hook 6)
- Opens with specific dosage numbers. "Most people take [X]mg. You actually need [Y]mg."

### comparison (Hook 9)
- "This [product A] vs. [product B] — here's what your pharmacist actually thinks."

## WEAK HOOK PATTERNS TO FLAG IN AUDIT

1. "Here is my pharmacist verdict on [product]" — label, not verdict. Replace with: "This is the best [product] for [condition]."
2. "Nobody talks about this — but [ingredient] in this product is the same as [competitor]" — positions as equivalent, not superior. Replace with superiority framing.
3. Any hook that announces what the video is about rather than making a claim or calling out an audience.
4. Any hook that starts with the product name before establishing a problem or making a bold claim.
5. Generic "as a pharmacist" opener without a specific claim attached.
