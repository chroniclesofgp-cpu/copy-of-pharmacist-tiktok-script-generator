# Design Ideas for Pharmacist TikTok Script Generator

## Context
This is a professional internal tool for a pharmacist TikTok Shop affiliate creator.
It needs to feel authoritative, clean, and fast — not a consumer app.
The user is a healthcare professional who wants to generate video scripts quickly.

<text>
**Idea 1: Clinical Command Center**

- **Design Movement:** Medical Brutalism meets Modern SaaS
- **Core Principles:** Authoritative, no-nonsense, information-dense, fast
- **Color Philosophy:** Deep navy (#0A1628) background with electric teal (#00D4AA) accents. White text. The teal signals "health" without being generic green. Evokes trust and precision.
- **Layout Paradigm:** Left sidebar for mode selection (Generate, Clone, Iterate), main content area with a two-column split (inputs left, output right). No centered hero sections.
- **Signature Elements:** Thin horizontal rule dividers, monospace font for generated scripts, pill badges for hook categories
- **Interaction Philosophy:** Every input immediately previews output. No "submit" friction.
- **Animation:** Subtle fade-in for generated content, typing cursor effect on script output
- **Typography System:** "Space Grotesk" for headings (bold, technical), "IBM Plex Mono" for script output (feels like a real script), "Inter" for body labels
</text>

**Idea 3: Dark Lab**

- **Design Movement:** Dark Mode SaaS / Developer Tool Aesthetic
- **Core Principles:** Focus-first, distraction-free, outputs feel premium

## Selected Design: Idea 1 — Clinical Command Center
Deep navy background, electric teal accents, two-column layout, monospace script output.
This feels most appropriate for a professional tool used by a pharmacist.

## Video Content Ideas Pipeline

### Idea — Medicube Instruction-Hook: "Why Kojic Acid Isn't Working For You"
**Logged:** May 2026
**Hook type:** Instruction-Correction
**Opening line concept:** "If you've tried kojic acid or turmeric and didn't see results — you're probably using it wrong."
**Core angle:** The science of why these ingredients require consistent use — cell turnover cycle is 28–40 days, melanin takes time to clear, most people quit too early or apply inconsistently. Pharmacist explains the mechanism (tyrosinase inhibition works upstream, the existing pigment still has to shed through natural cell turnover) so the viewer understands why patience is required and what "working correctly" actually looks like.
**Education beats:**
- Tyrosinase inhibitors stop NEW melanin from being produced — they do not bleach existing pigment
- Existing dark spots fade only as the skin cell turnover cycle completes (28–40 days per cycle)
- Most people see texture improvement before tone improvement — that is normal and expected
- Consistency matters more than concentration — skipping days resets the inhibition cycle
**Misdirection candidate:** "Everyone says you'll see results in 2 weeks. Honestly, the first cycle you're mostly just stopping new pigment from forming. The visible fading comes in cycles 2 and 3."
**Product tie-in:** Medicube Body Brightening Deodorant Stick (primary), can extend to Medicube kojic acid body wash and other SKUs in the same line — same education applies across all products.
**Scalability:** This exact script structure works for ANY kojic acid or turmeric product in the shop. The education section is product-agnostic — only the product introduction section changes.
