## Verified Corrections to Apply in Final Intel Documents

- **Serum:** Preserve the temporary, on-surface cosmetic positioning. The product-specific RCT supports an immediate visual colour change under the study protocol; it does not support permanent whitening, enamel repair, tartar prevention, or generic “sensitivity-free” superiority claims.
- **Serum:** Remove the incoming PDF’s use of PMID 32820846 as positive V34 support. It is a blue-covarine toothpaste study that found no significant whitening benefit versus conventional toothpaste.
- **V34 strips:** Do not use legacy PAP+ strip instructions or formula assumptions for the newer V34 strip SKU. The V34 ingredient list is now captured from a retailer label transcription corroborated by partial official seller / Target data. It does **not** include sorbitol, potassium citrate, hydroxyapatite, sodium saccharin, or peppermint oil as asserted in the incoming PDF.
- **PAP evidence:** Both PMID 38881836 and PMID 34940045 are mechanism / non-product-specific evidence, not grounds to claim V34 strips reproduce the study outcome or to promise no sensitivity / no enamel effects for every buyer.
- **PAP study limits, verified:** PMID 38881836 is a short communication on a professional PAP-complex procedure rather than an Hismile consumer-strip trial. PMID 34940045 is a laboratory enamel/stain model, tested six 10-minute PAP+ exposures, and was authored by Hismile Research Centre employees. It may support cautious PAP mechanism language only; it does not establish V34 strip outcomes or a universal no-sensitivity claim.
- **Bundle:** The incoming “Viral V34 Duo” is the official **Whitening Duo Bundle** at $39.99, containing V34 Colour Corrector Serum + **PAP+ Whitening Strips**. It is not a bundle of the newer V34 Whitening Strips. Remove all claims based on V34-strip formulation, 14-application count, V34-strip study figures, or unverified strip-serum synergy.
- **Bundle:** The official brand page establishes product contents and broad roles but does not state a combined step-by-step use sequence. Final bundle guidance must defer to each component’s official instructions rather than inventing a same-day order or schedule.
- **Exact TikTok bundle correction:** The supplied “Viral V34 Duo” maps to TikTok SKU `10060-VS_10018-CC`, which contains the serum plus the newer **V34 Whitening Strips**. It is a distinct current product configuration from the brand website’s $39.99 `Whitening Duo Bundle` (serum + PAP+ strips). The finalized bundle doc must identify the TikTok SKU as its primary source and treat site-bundle information only as a separate product-variant comparison.

The U.S. brand page independently supports daily / reapply-as-needed positioning but exposes only “30-seconds” marketing language; the finalized document must distinguish full label instructions from brand marketing copy and must not substitute the RCT cotton-bud protocol for normal product use.
- **Incoming strips PDF:** Remove product-specific claims of “sensitivity-free,” “no free radicals,” nHAp reinforcement, potassium-citrate desensitization, daily use, “Dry-Tuck” branding, peroxide superiority, and “PAP can’t reach enamel if plaque is present.” The uploaded listed formula does not match the verified V34 strip label, and the supplied PAP studies are not product-specific clinical proof.
