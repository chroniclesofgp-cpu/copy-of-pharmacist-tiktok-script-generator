How do you know if the probiotic you have is good quality and can be trusted?

I'm gonna give you a few tips on how to easily evaluate that just by looking at the label.

Does the label list the strain?

Most labels list the genus and the species, but the strain is a very important part because it defines what this probiotic can help with.

Take, for example, Lactobacillus plantarum.

Research shows strain 299v significantly decreases abdominal pain and bloating in IBS.

But strain MF1298 worsens IBS symptoms.

If a manufacturer doesn't list a strain, does this mean they don't know what strain they've isolated, or do they not value the importance of knowing the strain?

In order for a probiotic strain to provide benefit, it needs to be supplied at a minimum of one billion CFUs per strain, and that should be listed on the label.

A proprietary blend doesn't allow the consumer to see how many CFUs are per strain.

These are reasons to question the quality and manufacture of the probiotic.
