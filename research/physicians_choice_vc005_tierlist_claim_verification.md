## 2. BS50 Study — Exact Scope

| Study fact | Verification |
|---|---|
| Citation | [Garvey et al., *Gut Microbes* (2022), PMID 36269141](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36269141/) / [free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9590435/) |
| Design | Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel trial. |
| Participants and duration | 76 healthy adults; 2 × 10⁹ CFU BS50 daily for six weeks. |
| Product studied | **BS50 ingredient alone**, not the complete commercial Physician’s Choice Digestive Enzymes formula. |
| Primary/composite result | Improvement from baseline in the bloating, burping, and flatulence composite: **47.4% versus 22.2%** with placebo; *p* = .024. |
| Individual outcomes | Burping: 44.7% versus 22.2%, *p* = .041. Bloating: 31.6% versus 13.9%, *p* = .071. Flatulence: 47.4% versus 44.4%, *p* = .80. |

**Appeal and future-script boundary:** The video’s visual study title was accurately named, but wording that BS50 “was clinically tested and shown to significantly improve burping, bloating, and gas” is too broad. The study supports a significant **composite** result and an individual burping result; it does not show conventional statistical significance for individual bloating or flatulence, and it is not a trial of the complete linked product.

## 4. BS50 Preclinical Safety Source

The original script reference materials included [Brutscher et al. (2022), PMID 35630480](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35630480/), **“Preclinical Safety Assessment of *Bacillus subtilis* BS50 for Probiotic and Food Applications.”** The paper reports genomic screening, antibiotic-susceptibility testing, and Caco-2 cell analyses for the BS50 strain. It concludes that BS50 had a robust **preclinical** safety profile.

**Use boundary:** This is a relevant supplemental source for strain identity/safety context, but it is not a human efficacy trial, does not prove digestive-symptom improvement, and does not test the linked Physician’s Choice multi-ingredient product. It must not be used as evidence that the product works for bloating, burping, gas, nutrient absorption, or any other consumer result.

## 5. Appeal-Safe Factual Summary

The linked official listing supports the product’s 16-enzyme, probiotic/prebiotic, digestive-support, dosage, and quality facts. The visible BS50 study is a legitimate ingredient-level RCT, but its scope must be stated accurately. The appeal should not defend the broader wording as a complete-product trial or as proof that each individual symptom was significantly improved.
