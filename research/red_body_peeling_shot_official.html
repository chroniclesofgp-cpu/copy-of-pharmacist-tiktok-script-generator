    OVERVIEW

    Introducing Red Body Peeling Shot Cleanser, a revolutionary self-peeling body care solution. Crafted for easy home use, this powerful spray is infused with a striking deep red color, designed to extract troubles from within like a vampire.

    Enriched with AHA+BHA+PHA+LHA at 32%, our formula is designed to be delivered to the skin to dissolve deeply into thick body skin layers, effectively targeting stubborn skin issues and uneven texture. Encapsulated in fine particles, these potent ingredients combat the four main causes of body imperfections: Sebum, Oil, Dead Skin, and Trouble-causing Source.

    Through rigorous testing, Medicube has validated its ability to reduce pore clogging, a critical factor in skin concerns. Medicube's research demonstrates significant reductions in pore clogging, affirming the product's effectiveness in resolving root causes of body skin issues, ensuring smoother, clearer body skin.

    To use, simply apply to dry areas such as the back, chest, shoulders, and buttocks before showering. Leave on for 1 minute, then rinse off. Transform your skincare routine with Red Body Peeling Shot Cleanser, offering professional-quality body care in the comfort of your home.

    Our carefully crafted bottle features a 360-degree spray for easy application to hard-to-reach areas your hands can’t access. Evoke natural confidence and embrace smoother, clearer skin with every use!

    HOW TO USE
    • Patch Test: Before use, perform a patch test by applying a small amount on the inner arm or behind the ear to check for any adverse reactions.
    • 1. Targeted Application Spray onto dry areas like back, chest, shoulders, and buttocks from 15-20cm away.
    • 2. Rinse and Massage After 1 minute, rinse off with clean water. Gently massage reachable areas for best results.
      * The container is designed for 360-degree spraying, making it easy to apply to hard-to-reach areas without touching them.
      * Ensure the pump is clear of any remaining product to maintain effective spraying.
    • Intensive Care Method (1-2 times a week): 1. Apply a suitable amount to dry areas of concern before showering.
      2. Check the level of stimulation, leave for 1 minute, then rinse thoroughly with running water. You can also gently massage areas within reach.
    • Daily Care Method (daily use): 1. Spray the peeling shot 3-4 times onto a damp shower towel along with an appropriate amount of Red Body Wash to create lather.
      2. Rinse thoroughly during showering, taking care to avoid contact with eyes.
      3. Adjust the frequency and duration according to your skin type for optimal results.
    FAQ
    • Q. Who are Red Body Peeling Shot Cleanser recommended for? · Those concerned with persistent body skin imperfections, or troubled by rough areas on their body.
      · Anyone looking for a luxurious spa experience for at home body care to achieve smooth, soft skin and address concerns about excessive body oil and keratin.
    • Q. Do I need to perform a patch test before use? A. As this product contains high concentrations of active ingredients, apply a small amount to a cotton swab and test on the inside of your arm or behind your ear before use. Some users may experience a purging period such as temporary exfoliation or redness during the adaptation period. Please use with confidence according to your skin type.
    • Q. Should I rinse off after spraying on dry skin? A. This product is designed for body cleansing and should be rinsed off thoroughly with running water after use. It contains high concentrations of active ingredients, so please ensure to cleanse thoroughly.
    • Q. Can I use it for more than one minute? A. This product contains high concentrations of active ingredients. We recommend using it once every three days for one minute. Adjust the duration and frequency of use according to your skin type.
    • Q. What are the benefits of using Red Body Wash together with Red Body Peeling Shot Cleanser? A. Research shows significantly enhanced results compared to using them separately. In a human application trial, after just 1 minute of use, there was a 90.2% reduction in sebum (oil) and a 90.0% improvement in overall skin texture, including a 90.0% improvement specifically in the chest area skin texture.
      * Excerpt from Medicube Red Body Peeling Shot Cleanser Test Report and Survey. Results may vary.
    • Q. Can I use it on my face? A. This product is developed for body trouble use. For those who wish to treat facial trouble, please use "21% Red Succinic Acid Cleansing Booster Serum."
