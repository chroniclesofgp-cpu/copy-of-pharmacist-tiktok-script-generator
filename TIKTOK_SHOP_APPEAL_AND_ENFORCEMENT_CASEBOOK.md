## 1. How to Use and Update This Casebook

This document preserves enough detail to answer four future questions without relying on conversational memory: **what was posted, why it was flagged, exactly what was appealed, what evidence was available, and how TikTok resolved the case.** It is not a substitute for the controlling `TIKTOK_SHOP_HEALTH_BEAUTY_COMPLIANCE_PREFLIGHT.md` when preparing new content.

Every new enforcement event must receive a new `VC-###` record. Add the platform notice, the video/script identifier, the exact appeal text, supporting-document links or file locations, final platform outcome, and the date received. Preserve unknown fields as **“not preserved—awaiting source”** rather than reconstructing them from memory.

| Provenance label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **User-confirmed** | Directly reported or supplied by the creator, including screenshots and platform notices. |
| **Exact text preserved** | Quoted verbatim from a supplied screenshot, documented submission, or saved project record. |
| **Audit-derived** | Observed in the five-case enforcement audit; useful for risk analysis, but not a claim about TikTok’s proprietary trigger. |
| **Not preserved—awaiting source** | Details should be filled only after the original notice, submission, or Seller Support response is supplied. |

## 8. Evidence and Appeal-Package Checklist

For each new linked health, supplement, or beauty video, save the following before publishing. It supports better preflight review and a fact-grounded appeal if an action occurs.

| Evidence item | Why it belongs in the case file |
|---|---|
| Exact live TikTok PDP and package screenshots, dated | Proves the exact product variant and the words currently supporting each claim. |
| Claim map | Ties each spoken, text, caption, CTA, and visual claim to a source sentence on the PDP/package. |
| Claim-evidence upload manifest | For each potential attachment, record the exact statement, source URL, source scope (linked product / ingredient / comparator / general background), screenshot target, and what the source does **not** prove. |
| Final script, caption, hashtags, and on-screen text | Shows what was actually represented to viewers. |
| Original rendered video and product-cart screenshot | Preserves the final visual and commerce context. |
| Research links and evidence limits | Distinguishes product-level support from ingredient, animal, mechanism, or general background evidence. |
| Platform notice, pre-check result, appeal text, and outcome | Preserves the enforcement record exactly rather than relying on recall. |

> **Upload rule:** When an appeal accepts a limited number of JPEG/PNG screenshots, lead with the official linked PDP/package, then primary-study pages limited to their true scope, then official comparator sources only where they directly address the challenged comparison, and finally the original video/caption context. Screenshots are valid PNG/JPEG documents; do not manufacture composites. An attachment that contradicts an absolute spoken claim should not be used to defend that claim.

### Exact Claims Recorded by TikTok

TikTok’s notice quotes the video’s comparison and product explanation, including the following material statements: a standard probiotic “doesn't give you anything to help break down food or deal with any immediate symptoms”; simethicone “only treats the symptoms and does nothing for the problem actually causing them”; a digestive-enzyme complex provides broad mealtime support and covers proteins, carbs, fats, dairy, and plant foods; the Physician’s Choice product has 16 enzymes, prebiotic fiber, and BS50; BS50 was “tested and shown to significantly improve burping, bloating and gas”; one capsule is taken daily with the largest meal; and the product is vegan, non-GMO, third-party tested, and made in the U.S.

The notice also cites the on-screen study title:

> “The probiotic Bacillus subtilis BS50 decreases gastrointestinal symptoms in healthy adults: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial. Sean M Garvey et al. Gut Microbes. 2022”

### Complete Video-Audit Boundary

The full video was watched and separately transcribed. Directly observed elements include: A/B/C/S tier assignments; product images for lactase, a standard probiotic, simethicone, and Physician’s Choice Digestive Enzymes; the physical Physician’s Choice bottle and capsule; the persistent `Results may vary` text; the 2022 BS50 study-title popup; an official product-link CTA with a physical downward point; and sale/price-rise urgency wording. The video did not show before/after imagery, altered body imagery, a diagnosis, or a guarantee.

### Verified Source Context and Claim Boundary

The current official Physician’s Choice product page and the live official TikTok Shop listing both document the 16-enzyme formula, probiotic/prebiotic blend including *Bacillus subtilis* BS50®, the product’s digestive/bloating-support positioning, 1-capsule mealtime directions, and relevant quality attributes. The displayed [BS50 study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36269141/) is a six-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of **the BS50 ingredient**, not the complete Physician’s Choice product. Its statistically significant endpoint was the composite improvement rate for bloating, burping, and flatulence (47.4% versus 22.2% placebo); individual burping was statistically significant, while individual bloating did not reach conventional statistical significance. This distinction must be preserved in the appeal and in any future revision.

### Appeal Outcome and Evidence Lesson

The user confirms that the appeal was approved after submission of the 491-character appeal and ten-slot claim-mapped screenshot package. This is an individual outcome, not proof that every disputed statement was endorsed or that the exact video is a reusable compliant template. The outcome-screen wording is **not preserved—awaiting source**.

VC-004 and VC-005 are both user-confirmed approved appeals that included focused supporting material. VC-001 and VC-002 had no supporting documents in their preserved in-app appeal records and were denied. This is a useful **two-versus-two operational correlation**, not proof that documentation alone caused approval. Future appeals should still pair concise factual context with official linked-product facts, accurately scoped research, and the original video/caption context—without submitting attachments that overstate or contradict the claim at issue.

The appeal must not describe the BS50 ingredient study as a complete-product clinical trial or state that all three individual symptoms were each significantly improved.
