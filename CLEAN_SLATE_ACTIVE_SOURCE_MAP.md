## 2. Active Writing Standard

The project retains the restored pre-trial framework and creator-analysis system. A script must follow the selected, source-validated framework’s actual hook, section order, pacing, visuals, proof behavior, product placement, and CTA mechanics.

The sole standing protection applied during writing is **sentence-level qualification**. Use natural qualified language where appropriate, such as “may help,” “helps support,” “can help,” “is used to help,” or “has been studied for.” Do not replace the viewer payoff, pharmacist explanation, direct benefit, proof strategy, pain point, or framework mechanics with defensive narration. The creator will make final filming, visual, caption, and posting decisions.

## 3. Just-in-Time Source Loading

Load only the sources required for the immediate task.

### Context-Reset Control

If working context has been compressed, reset, or otherwise cannot reliably retain the full text of a critical document, **stop the task at that point**. Reload the governing document(s), source logs, product facts, original media/source material, and quality or protocol controls required for the next action before analyzing, documenting, or drafting. A prior summary or recollection does not substitute for a fresh read of a critical active source.

For example, creator-video analysis requires its original video and source log; product intelligence requires the current research protocol and primary product sources; script drafting requires the six full writing references and the approved fact-only product record.
